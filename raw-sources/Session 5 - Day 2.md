The party was face to face with a creature that probed each of their minds, a [[Nothic]]. The party, disguised by they Redbrand cloaks, learned that the Nothic was essentially a guard dog, paid with food to protect the hideout. 

It learned via probing that the party weren't actually Redbrands, but the group was able to barter safe passage for the price of food given to the Nothic. Petals decided to try and fly down into the crevice and found the Nothics treasure. She had to work quick, but was able to get away unseen with a spell scroll and two potions.

Further along, the party found a room with 2 bugbears and a goblin inside minding their own business. With their Redbrand disguises the group was able to convince the Goblinoids they were friendly and avoided combat with them, in fact learning from them that [[Delrun#Glasstaff|Glass Staff]] was in fact there in the hideout.

The party made their way further in the hideout to find a room filled with 4 very drunk Redbrands. With some quick thinking, Arturas, Elaineke and Petals played of characters to distract the gang members. However the ruse didn't last and the group was forced to enter into open combat. Luckily since they were incredibly drunk, the party was able to make short work of the 4 of them.

It was at this point the party found themselves in Glasstaffs chambers. The party inspected most of the room and found a potion of invisibility as well as some notes regarding [[Apostles#Nezznar The Black Spider|The Black Spider]] and proof that Glasstaff was in fact the same person as Iarno Albreck.

They then made theri way in to Glasstaffs bedroom and found a secret passage left ajar after he made a hasty escape. The party followed down a passage that led back to the main entrance where they encountered and killed Glasstaff. They then were able to take his staff and went out back to Delrun with their proof of victory over the Redbrands and of Lord Albrecks whereabouts...
