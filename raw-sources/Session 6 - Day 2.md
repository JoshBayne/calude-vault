Before leaving the hideout, the party went back to check the magically locked door to find that it wasn't locked at all and was a fake spell. Inside they found 2 Redbrand guards watching over 3 new slaves. The party fought to rescue [[Delrun#Dendrar Family|Nars Nilsa and Mirna Dendrar]], while learning that their Father and Husband, Thel, had been taken before and was missing.

The party made their way back to [[Delrun]] with the proof that Lord Albreck had become a traitor to the crown and had allied himself with The Black Spider as well as three shaken but safe humans. 

Sildar was in disbelief, but wrote back to The Hand station in Aronoc to update them on the story. He then offered the party a new job if they were willing to take it, it would give the party a chance to learn more about Wave Echo Cave while also being another chance at good pay and loot. 

The party took the job and learned that they needed to try and find a group of Goblins that had allied with The Black Spider and clear them out of [[Cragmaw Castle]]. The exact whereabouts of the castle were unknown to anyone in town though, so they were directed to the ruins of [[Thundertree]] to find the [[Thundertree#Reidoth|Reidoth]] who knew the area intimately.

The party decided to take a nights rest back at the Inn to recharge and refuel before continuing the next day...
