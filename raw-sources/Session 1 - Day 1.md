Adventure: [[LMoP]]


Petals, Taegan, and Ginny all found each other taking the same job from a dwarven gentleman by the name of [[Delrun#^371317|Gundren Rockseeker]]. They had been tasked with taking a cart of supplies from the city of [[Aronoc]] to the small frontier town of [[Delrun]]. Along the path, the three met Artuas and Elaineke as well.

After greetings had been exchanged, the party noticed up ahead that horses lay in the road with items scattered about. Upon getting closer to inspect, the party was assaulted by a small goblin ambush. After the scuffle, a few belongings related to Gundren and another unknown individual were found.

After some debate the party decided to follow the path left by the goblins, where they found their hideout, [[Cragmaw Hideout]]. The party decided to go in, in an attempt to find Gundren.

The party made their way into the cave and found dozens more goblins as well as a wolf pair.

It took some teamwork, and a healthy amount of luck, but the party was about to find a shortcut to the commanders section of the cave not far from the entrance. Inside they found a bugbear commanding officer. 

The party was able to narrowly defeat and kill Klarg before they took a moment to rest and catch their breath...
