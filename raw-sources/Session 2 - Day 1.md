After narrowly escaping death and making new wolf allies, the party caught their breath and headed backwards through the cave. Goblin enemies that had witnessed the death of their leader ran off alerting their allies that all was lost and to run away. 

The party used this chaos to find a secluded goblin den that had a lone human male tied up in the darkness. He was beaten within an inch of his life, all his belongings stolen save his underwear, and was left to die by the fleeing goblins.

His name was [[Delrun#Sildar Hallwinter|Sildar Hallwinter]], a member of [[The Abiding Hand]] who had been tasked with finding [[Delrun#Glasstaff|Iarno Albreck]] as he had gone silent recently and stopped reporting back to headquarters about his new position in [[Delrun]]. On the road in to the town, Sildar encountered his old friend [[Delrun#^371317|Gundren Rockseeker]] who informed him that the [[Delrun#Rockseeker Brothers|Rockseeker Brothers]] had found what they believed to be something of great historical value. 

The party was skeptical at first, since they were on edge after nearly dying, but they agreed to take the man back in to town for the reward that Sildar offered.

After a few hours the party arrived in Delrun just as the sleepy town was starting to wind down, and because of that the group decided to stay the night in the [[Delrun#Stonehill Inn|Stonehill Inn]] to rest before the next day...
