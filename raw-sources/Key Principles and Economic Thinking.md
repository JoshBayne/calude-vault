# 1. Economics is concerned with making choices with limited resources. Choices are necessary because resources are scarce.

## **RESOURCE** 
Anything that can be used to produce something else.

## **SCARCITY** 
Unlimited wants clash with limited resources.

	
# 2. The true cost of something is its opportunity cost.

## **OPPORTUNITY COST** 
is the highest-valued alternative that must be given up to get it.

## **TRADEOFF** 
an exchange—giving up one thing to get something else.


# 3. Specialization leads to gains for all involved.

# 4. People respond to incentives, both good and bad

## **INCENTIVES** 
rewards and penalties that motivate behavior

### Positive Incentives - encourage behavior
### Negative Incentives - discourage behavior


# 5. "How Much" is a decision at the margin.
## Thinking on the Margin - Marginal Analysis
Comparing all the relevant alternatives *systematically* and *incrementally*

### **Marginal Benefit (MB)** 
what you gain when you get one more unit of something

### **Marginal Cost (MC)**
the additional cost of a one-unit increase in an activity.


Decision Making Rule
	MB>MC - Do It!
	MC>MB - Don't Do It!
	Goal: MB=MC
