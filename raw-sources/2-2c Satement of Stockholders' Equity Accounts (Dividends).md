 # Satement of Stockholders' Equity Accounts (Dividends)
 The debit and credit rules for recording dividends are based on the effect of dividends on stockholders'/owners equity (retained earnings). Since dividends decrease owners equity (retained earnings), the dividends account is increased by debits. Likewise, the dividends account is decreased by credits. Thus the rules of debit and credit for the dividends account are as follows. 

#### Dividends Accounts
Debit for increases
Credit for decreases

*This is fed into the Debit side of Retained Earnings*
