Discovered after the deployment of the JWST in the early 2020's. While unknown in origin, human scientists, researchers, and engineers were able to successfully utilize their abilities to warp ships and people from one gate to the next to help grow the borders of humanity to as previously unthought of heights.

Some feel that the gates are religious in nature. The [[Unitary Church of the Gates of Gods Glory]] feels that the gates are in fact a road to God, created to be used when humanity had reached the maturity required to meet Him.

Still others worry about what happened to the previous, unknown, creators of these Gates? Why did they disappear? Perhaps from some catastrophe or something else terrifying.

In any event, the undeniable benefit that the Gates have had on humanity are innumerable.
