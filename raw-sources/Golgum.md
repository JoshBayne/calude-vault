An [[Apostles|Apostle]] in service to the [[Sovereigns]]. 

A suave, confident, young looking Elven man, black hair slicked back over his oval shaped head. His eyes a piercing amber color, and a smile that exudes warmth and comfot.
True form is that of a hideous man-bat that can use his venom to turn others into vampire slaves. Those slaves can in turn others into zombies that follow their masters orders.

Currently hiding out as a priest in a city of [[Aronoc]] in the temple of [[Lathander]] as cover. Using his vampire powers of persuasion he is able to feed under the cover of priestly duties to the ill and infirm. Currently has a plot to turn Aronoc into a society of slaves under his command.
