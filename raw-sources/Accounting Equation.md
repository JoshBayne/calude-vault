## Accounting Equation
The resources owned by busniess are called **assets**
The rights of creditors are the debts of business and are called **liabilities**
The rights of owners are called **equity**
- Since stockholders own a corp, equity is called **stockholders equity**
- For a proprietorship, partnership, or llc, equity is called **owners equity**

***Accounting Equation*** is $Assets = Liabilities + Stockholders\phantom{0}Equity$
