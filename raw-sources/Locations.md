 ### Crimson World
 Home of the original Dogu, millenia of expansion and destruction of the natural world has caused the planet to become one mega city. No green exists anymore, and the planet is awash with Crimson and Gold. The sky looks as though it is on fire from the pollutants in the air and the peoples must stay inside so as to not be burned from the radiation from the nearest star, all because the protective layers of the atmosphere have long since vanished. A testament to the unending greed of capitalism and profits.
