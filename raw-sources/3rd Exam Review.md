Accounting Equation
Assets = Liability + Equity


Prepaid Expenses (asset) - will be recognized as ex0pense in the future

Accrued Revenues (asset) - revenue earned but not yet billed

Unearened revenue (liability) - pre-paid but no service rendered yet

Accrued expenses (liability) - expenses incurred but not yet paid

## Accrued Expenses
- matching priniciple followed
- expense acct debited, liability credited
- when paid, the liability will be debited and cash is credited
- unpaid but earned expenses like wages need to be accrued as of the balance sheet date

## Contingent liability
- must be probable and estimatable
- must be recorded and disclosed
- examples
	- product warranties and lawsuits
- product warranty expense recorded when item is sold
