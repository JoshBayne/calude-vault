Born from the ashes of the old Earth United Nations. 
The UIC has been the predominate governmental body overseeing humanity and her worlds for the past half millennia. Ostensibly designed to benefit all of mankind, the UIC has become a bloated mess of bureaucracy, completely incapable of stopping the rampant corruption within its upper echelons. 

Headquartered on [[Mars]], the UIC acts as peacekeepers, with its [[Navy]] presence felt across every world humans occupy. 

Led by the [[Council of 13]] who employ [[Headhunters]] as their private peacekeeping force.
