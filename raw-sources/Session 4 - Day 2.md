The party stood outside the [[Delrun#Tresendar Manor Former Redbrand Hideout|Redbrand Hideout]], ready to go in and defeat the gang to both collect payment, and try to find out more about Wave Echo Cave.

As they entered, the party found themselves in an empty chamber, with only one noticeable door leading to another area. The eerie silence of it all disturbed the party, but they pressed on.

The next room was the crypt for the former manor, where as they strayed a bit to close to the coffins, they were attacked by reanimated skeletons. It surprised them, but they pulled through without many wounds. Afterwards they inspected the area and found a door that seemed to be locked by some form of magic, as well as another that opened into an armory of sorts.

Investigating the armory revealed some Redbrand cloaks and a hidden passage that opened into a small narrow hallway. Moving through the passage, the party found themselves in a large chamber, split by a large fissure that ran down the center. They pressed on and were accosted by a creature they had never seen before, crawling towards them from the darkness...
