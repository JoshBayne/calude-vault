The party made their way to [[Thundertree]] in an attempt to find the [[Thundertree#Reidoth|Reidoth]] and make their way on to [[Cragmaw Castle]].

Once they arrived, they found the place in absolute ruins with signs posted saying zombies were an issue. As they trekked further in, they came across a hut with a woman inside, as well as another hut with Dragon worshippers surrounding an egg they had.

On further inspection, the party found that [[Gary]] was among the cultists, looking again for somewhere to belong. Gary informed the party that they had stolen the egg from a nearby dragon and didn't quite know what to do with it. 

The party convinced Gary to join them to safety and avoid the wrath of a dragon. [[Venomfang]] then made an appearance and inspected the party on their way out, determining that none of them had the egg. 

As the party left the area, they heard the flames and cries of cultists as Venomfang retook her egg. 

The party then coerced Gary into going back to Delrun and moved on with their quest.

As they approached the [[Cragmaw Castle]] they heard the sound of goblin voices at an entrance...
