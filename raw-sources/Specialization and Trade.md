#econ2301   

#### Trade Creates Value
- Trade makes people better off when preferences differ

#### Trade increases productivity
- Through specialization
- Through Comparative Advantage

### Absolute Advantage
- One country can produce more of a good than another country

### Comparative Advantage
- One country's [[Opportunity Cost]] is lower than another country's

![[Pasted image 20211207210027.png]]

##### Opportunity cost of smoothies
- Liz: 30/30 = 1 salad
- Joe: 30/6 = 5 salads
	- Liz has the comparative advantage in producing smoothies

##### Opportunity cost of salad
- Liz: 30/30 = 1 smoothie
- Joe: 6/30 = 1/5 smoothie
	- Joe has a comparative advantage in producing salads
see: [[Opportunity Cost#Opportunity Cost is a ratio]]

![[Pasted image 20211207210542.png]]

##### Terms of Trade
- Smoothies for salads
	- Liz: 1:1
	- Joe: 1:5
##### Acceptable Range
- 1 smoothie for 1-5 salads

![[Pasted image 20211207210715.png]]

##### Achieving Gains from Trade
Terms of trade: 1 smoothie for 2 salads
- Liz and Joe trade:
	- Liz sells Joe 10 smoothies and buys 20 salads
	- Joe sells Liz 20 salads and buys 10 smoothies


![[Pasted image 20211207210846.png]]

Liz and Joe each produce at Point A on their PPFs
Liz and Joe specialize in producing the good in which they have a comparative advantage
Liz and Joe trade salads and smoothies at a price of 2 salads per smoothie
Liz and Joe consume at point C, which is outside their PPFs
- Both gain from specialization and trade
