## Minimum Take Aways
Financial statements, prepared according to US generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP), must include the following:
- **Balance Sheet** - may be called Statement of Financial Position or similar title.
- **Income Statement** - may be called Statement of Activities or similar title.
- **Comprehensive Income Statement** - may be combined with the income statement or follow the income statement. It includes the "other comprehensive income" items.
- **Statement of Changes in Equity** - or similar title. Focus in book is on Retained Earnings portion of statement of changes in equity
- **Cash Flow Statement** - or similar title

The Financial Accounting Standards Board (**FASB**) is the accounting standard setting body for US GAAP. Pronounced "fas-B"

The International Accounting Standards Board (**IASB**) is the accounting standard setting body for international standards which are used by over half of the nations in the world for their publicly traded companies. Pronounced "I-A-S-B"

The Securities Exchange Commission (**SEC**) regulates the opublicly traded companies. Financial statements of publicly traded companies must be files with the SEC. The annual rport is filed on form **10-K** for a US company and form 20-F if the company is a foreign company listed on a US exchange.

The accounting equation, Assets=Liabilities+Equity, is the basis of the universally used double entry accounting system. It is based on the debits equal credits system.

Transactions are recorded in accounts and every type of account has a "normal sign"

Any account balance that is opposite the normal/expected sign is put in brackets on a published financial statement.

Owners of corporations are referred to as shareholders or stockholders.

Owners of an LLC are referred to as **members**

The **cost** (or historical cost) concept means that transactions are recorded at the actual amount paid or to be received from an economic activity

The "**fair value**" concept is used for certain assests or liabilities and the balances in those accounts are adjusted to the current market prices as of the balance sheet date.

**Dividends** are a distribution of profits by a corporation to the shareholders.

GAAP statements must be on the **accrual basis**. Strongly recommend that "cash basis" be only used for tax purposes. Even the smalled business needs information that can only be found in accrual basis for accounting system reports.

The **matching concept** requires that all expenses related to eaarning revenue recognized must be recorded in the same reporting period.
