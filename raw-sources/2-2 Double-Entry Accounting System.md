# Double-Entry Accounting System
All businesses use what is called the *double-entry accouting system*[^1]. This system is based on the [[Accounting Equation]] and requires:
	- Every business transaction to be recorded in at least 2 accounts
	- The total debits recorded for each transaction to be equal to the total credits recorded
The double-entry accounting system also has specific *rules of debit and credit*[^2] for recording transactions in the accounts.

[^1]: A system of accounting for recording transactions, based on recording increases and decreases in accounts so that debits equal credits.
[^2]: In the double-entry accounting system, specific rules for recording debits and credits based on the type of account.
