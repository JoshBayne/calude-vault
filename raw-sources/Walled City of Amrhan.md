Protected city near the Eastern Border of [[Arian]].

Haunted Gate - Location where in a battle long since past the Kings Army held off an Orc invasion. Said to be haunted by the ghosts of those that died there millennia ago. Nobody actually believes this, until they see a ghost that is.

Trade Town - Center of commerce for Amrhan, dense with shops of every sort

Mist Gate - Named for the morning mist that accumulates here from the nearby lake, southern entrance to the city

Castle Olgrach - Ancestral home to Duke Bramrir. Seat of power for the city.

Priests Lawn - Green area full of lush vegetation neslted in front of the castle. Used by residents as a public park for recreation.
