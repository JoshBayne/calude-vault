MC is a driven future space captain, introduce him as a character and the world around us
MC works in a motel near a star port, using the copious downtime between anyone coming in to study things like piloting and orbital mechanics. Something like Ethan Hawke in Gattaca.

One night a strange man comes in with a young child that he acts incredibly cagey about. When MC threatens to call authorities for a suspected trafficking case, the man reveals he is a researcher for the government who had become disgusted with the way the government was using AIs as a form of slave labor.
