# Vocabulary
- Speaking Purpose - Why are you speaking?

- Ceremonial Speech - ritual events where a speech is given (wedding, funeral, promotion) to mark the significance of the occasion

- Demonstration Speech - Teaching or guidance speaking, may be hands on learning. *ex* Coaching a skill 

- Epidictic Speech - Awards speechs, and speeches of refelction and gratitude (differ from ceremonial in that they are **always** framed around gratitude) closest tied to sentimental emotion and more heartfelt. Can be intimate in that you have a small audience

- Informative Speech - To educate on a topic. Differs from a demonstration speech in that its a pure info dump as a passive listener.

- Forensic Speech - Technically addresses the past, but is framed in terms of deliberation. Courtroom deliberation as an example. Crafting an argument on events that have already occured.

- Deliberative Speech - Speaking on something that has not occured. Policy speeches like a political speech on the campaign or proposing a new bill or in the workplace to propose a change.

- Persuasive Speech - To influence the audience. Speaking in the present tense. "Influence now". Gives the promise of something new.
