When we have more than one linear equation that we are trying to solve simultaneously, we have a system of linear equations. A solution to the system is a point that solves every equation. Considere the system below
x-3y=7
2x+5y=-8
Both of these equations represent a line in the xy plane. Graphically the solution to this system would be the point where the lines intersect, as that point would make both equaitons true.
The two lines appear to intersect at the point (1,-2). 

When we a re dealing with 2 lines, there are 3 possible oucomes. First there could be exactly one solution. Second, there could be no solution (lines are parallel). Third, there could be infinite solutions, the two lines are the same. In the third case we say the system is dependent.

Graphs can only get us so far. Even in three variabvles, it is hard to see a solution as the graph would now be in three dimensions. If there are more than 3 variables, then its not even possible to graph the equations. So we need to solve in other ways.

One way is through substitution. We solve one equation for one of the variables, and sub for the same varible in the other equation. Now we only have one variable and we can solve as we have previously.

2x-4(2x-9)=6

-6x+36=6

y=1

(x,y)=(5,1)

A nurse has two solutions that contain different concentrations of a certain medication. One is 20% concentration and the other is a 5% concentration. How many cubic centimeters of each should be mixed to obtain 10cc of an 11% solution?

x= amount of 20% solution
y= amount of 5% solution
Goal : 10 cc of 11% solution

10cc total : x+y=10
11% solution : 0.2x+.05y=1.1 (.11 times 10)

y=10-x
