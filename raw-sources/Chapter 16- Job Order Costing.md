Job order cost syste provides product costs for each quantity of product that is manufactured.

Job order cost systems are often used by companies that manufacture custom products for customers or batches of similar products
- ex. Apparel, guitars

While a job is still in the prodction process, it is part of Work in Process inventory.
When completed, Finished Goods invenory
When sold, costs become COGS

The materials account in the general ledger is a controlling account. A seperate account for each type of material is maintained in a materials ledger.

Material ledger account
- Increases (debits) are based on receiving reports, which is supported by suppliers invoice
- Decreases (credits) are based on material requisitions

Factory overhead costs are normally allocated or applied to jobs using a predetermined factory overhead rate
This predetermined rate is computed as follows:
Predetermined Factory Overhead rate = (estimated total overhead costs)/(estimated activity base)

Activity base
- direct labor hours
- direct labor cost
- machine hours

Example - Assume the Legend Guitas estimated the total FOH cost as $50,000 for the year and the activity base as 10,000 direct labor hours. The predetermined FOH rate is:
FOH Rate = (50,000)/(10,000)=$5 per direct labor hour

Factory overhead account is:
- increased (debited) for actual overhead costs
- decreased (credited) for the applied overhead
Actual applied overhead usually differs beceause actual overhead costs are normally different from estimated costs

If applied overhead is less than actual incurred overhead, FOH account will have a debit balance
- Underapplied factory overhead
If applied is more than actual, FOH will have a credit balance
- Overapplied FOH

During the year, the balance in the FOH account is carried forward and reported as a deferred debit or credit on teh manthly (interim) balance sheets
Anay balance in the FOH account should not be carried in to next year
- this balance only applies to the current year operations
Balance of FOH account at year end is disposed of by transferring it to COGS account
- if there is a debit (underapplied) balance then COGS is debited and FOH is credited
- If there is a credit balance, COGS is credited and FOH is debited
