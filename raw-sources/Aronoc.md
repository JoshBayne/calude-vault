# "City of Lights"

Along the coastal region of [[Arian]]

Broken up into 5 districts.  

## Dwarven district 
Fabrication and manufacturing center of the city.

---

## Trade district
Massive marketplace where commerce is king.

---

## North district
Seat of power for the city of Aronoc. All political dealings are done here with the cities elders. Home to the affluent and connected.

### Measured Knightstead
Magical school in the new district of Aronoc.

#### Zaffrab Symphony
A middle aged male gnome. He is somewhat tiny and bony, and has green eyes and a neckbeard, with tan skin. The most notable physical trait of Zaffrab is that he has incredibly frizzy hair.

Zaffrab is currently the only active teacher in The Measured Knightstead, making for a lonely existence during the quarantine as none of his students are able to come to classes.
Inquisitive and curious, Zaffrab won't pass up an opportunity to use his brain to solve any perceived puzzle, sometimes to his detriment. Recently he has taken up alchemy and potioncrafting as a hobby to expand his knowledge further.

---

## Old District
Poorer more rundown area at the southern end of the city. True population unknown due to the large amounts of orphans and transients.  

### The Soggy Alchemist
