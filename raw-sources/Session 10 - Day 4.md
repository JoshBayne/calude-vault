The party had finally cleared out the majority of Wave Echo Cave and were now face to face with the villain behind all of this. [[Apostles#Nezznar The Black Spider|The Black Spider]]. 

As the battle began, the party made short work of two of Nezznars bugbear guards and several hidden giant spiders with a well placed Fireball from Arturas. With them out of the way it was a simple matter of finishing the Black Spider and making off with the hidden treasure of the cave. 

Best laid plans however... As the party thought they had laid the final blow, Nezznar slammed his staff onto the ground and cocooned himself in ethereal black webs, all before emerging reborn as a spider monstrosity, half Drow, Half Spider.

The party engaged for what felt like an eternity, facing several close calls like when Taegan fell unconcious, as well as highs like when Ginny climbed atop the foul creature to get up close and personal.

In the end, the party was victorious with the final blow being dealt by Petals with a simple but incredibly effective use of her Magic Missile darts.

When the dust settled and the party was safe, they began the looting. Ginny found herself in possession of 2 massive emeralds from the statue that adorned this room, and Taegan found himself the new owner of Nezznars spider staff. After rescuing [[Delrun#^14c30b|Nundro Rockseeker]] the party used his knowledge to find both the fabled Lightbringer mace, as well as the Dragonguard armor from ages past.

The party also learned the name of a mysterious person that Nezznar was working with, [[Golgum]]. 

Now that the party is finished with the cave, what trials await them next...?
