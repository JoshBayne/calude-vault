1. Vocabulary is important
2. Accounting systes prepared under GAAP are based on the double entry accounting system and use the accrual basis (accounts receivable/payable)
3. Every accounting system starts with a chart of accounts
4. When economic events occur that affect the assets, liabilities or equity balances, these events are journalized in journal entries by debiting and crediting the accounts listed on the chart of accounts

|              | Debit | Credit |
| ------------ | ----- | ------ |
| Cash         | xxx   |        |
| Loan Payable |       | xxx    | 

5. After the entries are journalized, each of the debits and credits are posted (recorded) in the general ledger in that account
6. T accounts can be used to represent the accoutns in the general ledger. The general ledger summarizes but also retains the detail of the transactions posted to each account
