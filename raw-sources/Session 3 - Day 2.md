The party woke from their night at the Stonehill Inn to realize that Sildar had made his way to [[Delrun#Harbin Wester|Harbin Wester]], the mayor of Delruns, office to continue his work with The Hand.

The party used this opportunity to deliver their goods to [[Delrun#Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]] and receive their payment. Afterwards they explored town for a bit trying to find the mayors office, coming across several individuals looking for help. Among them included a Sister Graele, an Elf cleric wanting help for her mission with [[The Harpers]].

The party also found the [[Delrun#Lionshield Coster|Lionshield Coster]], a vendor of armor and weapons. They also found out that [[Delrun#Barthen's Provisions|Barthen's Provisions]] sold adventuring gear as well as other normal goods.

After a bit of shopping, the party made their way to the mayors office to speak to Sildar about how to collect the reward he offered for his rescue, as well as to get more info about Wave Echo Cave.

Sildar informed the party that while the Rockseeker brothers may have found the cave, they had been kidnapped by an organization known as the [[Redbrand|Redbrands]]. The mayor begged for their removal from the area.

The party decided to make their way to the [[Delrun#Tresendar Manor Former Redbrand Hideout|Redbrand Hideout]] in an attempt to rid the town of the nuisance...
