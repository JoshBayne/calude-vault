# Delrun
Small frontier town some miles inland from the coastal portion of the [[Kings Road]] nestled in the [[Emerald Wood]] south of [[Aronoc]].

Recently was freed from [[Redbrand]] control by the party.

Was created before the [[Delrundian Pact]] and the current town was built over the ruins of the previous ancient city.

Recently however, settlers from Aronoc and [[Vindun]] have started the hard work of rebuilding. A bustling settlement has sprung up, home to farmers, woodcutters, traders and prospectors drawn to the area by stories of precious metals in the foothills of The Crags. Unfortunately, a number of bandits have come as well, taking advantage of the fact that the area has no Abiding Hand to protect the people. A gang known as the [[Redbrand]]s has taken control of Delrun for the past two months, extorting everyone in town. They're led my a mysterious figure known to the townsfolk as Glasstaff.

Nearby is the [[Wave Echo Cave]], a site of powerful magics

## 1. Locations

### Townmaster's Hall
Delrun has no functioning government, but the townsfolk elect someone to serve as townmaster each year. The townmaster serves as a judge in minor disputes and keeps any records that need to be kept. 

The townmaster's hall has a small but serviceable jail in the cellar. The jail consists of two cells, and Harbin carries keys to the cell doors.

#### Harbin Wester
The current townmaster is a male human banker named Harbin Wester—a fat, pompous old fool. Completely intimidated by the Redbrands.

#### Sildar Hallwinter
Member of [[The Abiding Hand]] tasked with finding Iarno Albreck. Currently staying in [[Delrun]] while Rockseeker Brothers restart mining operations.

---

### Stonehill Inn
This modest inn has six rooms for rent. If the characters decide to stay here, see "Food, Drink, and Lodging" in the rulebook for pricing. (The characters' other alternative for lodging is to camp outside the town, or to persuade a farmer such as Doran Edermath or Qelline Alderleaf to let them sleep in a hayloft.)

#### Toblen Stonehill
Toblen is a native of the town of Triboar to the east. He came to Delrun to prospect, but soon realized that he knew a lot more about running an inn than he did about mining. The new town offered a good opportunity to become established.

---

### Barthen's Provisions
Barthen's is the biggest trading post in Delrun. Its shelves stock most ordinary goods and supplies, including backpacks, bedrolls, rope, and rations. The place is open from sunup to sundown. Barthen's does not stock weapons or armor, but characters can purchase other adventuring gear here, with the exception of items that cost more than 25 gp. Characters in need of weapons or armor are directed to the Lionshield Coster (see that section).

#### Elmar Barthen
A lean and balding human male shopkeeper of fifty years with a kindly manner. He employs a couple young clerks (Ander and Thistle) who help load and unload wagons, and who wait on customers when Barthen isn't around.

---

### Lionshield Coster 
This building is owned by the [[Lionshields]], a merchant company based in the city of [[Yartar]], over a hundred miles to the east. They ship finished goods to Delrun and other small settlements throughout the region, but this outpost has been hard hit by banditry. The most recent Lionshield caravan due in Delrun never arrived. (It was attacked and its cargo captured by the Cragmaw goblins.)

In a back room, Linene keeps a supply of armor and weapons, all of which are for sale to interested buyers. Linene has a few scruples, however, and won't sell weapons to anyone she thinks might be a threat to the town. Among those with whom she refuses to do business are the Redbrands. She warns the characters that the ruffians are trouble and advises them to avoid the Sleeping Giant tap house.

#### Linene Graywind
The master of the Delrun, a sharp-tongued human woman of thirty-five. She knows that bandits have raided Lionshield caravans, but she doesn't know who is responsible.
Coerced into sexual advances by the party and is currently on leave in [[Port of Biscayne]] for an indefinite leave of absence over the ordeal.

---

### Edermath Orchard
Daran is concerned about the Redbrands, and he would like to see a group of adventurers teach the ruffians a lesson. He tells the characters that it's time someone took a stand against the Redbrands' leader, Glasstaff. Daran knows the Redbrands hang around the Sleeping Giant tap house, but he can also tell the characters that the main Redbrand safe house lies under Tresendar Manor, the ruin at the east edge of town. (See the "Tresendar Manor" section for more information.)

#### Daran Edermath
Daran Edermath is a retired adventurer who lives in a tidy little cottage beside an apple orchard. A fit, silver-haired half-elf well over a hundred years old, Daran is a fighter who served as a marshal and herald for many years in the lands of the Yertle Coast, far to the southeast before having his mind wiped for unknown reasons by a wizard. Upon retiring, he returned to Arian, his original home.

---

### Tresendar Manor (Former Redbrand Hideout)
Run down and abandoned manor on the outskirts of Delrun. The basement was used as the base of operations by the Redbrands until being cleared out by the party. Currently sits unoccupied though the offer is open for the party to purchase the home. 

### Glasstaff
Real Name is Iarno Albreck, a former member of [[The Abiding Hand]]. 

Former colleague of Sildar Hallwinter.

Lieutenant of [[Apostles#Nezznar The Black Spider|The Black Spider]], killed by the party in the basement hideout of the manor.
