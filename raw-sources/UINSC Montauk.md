[[UIC]] ship commissioned over 200 years ago to explore and chart deep space. Made several successful voyages before mysteriously disappearing. In a first of its kind incident, the Montauk made her initial jump into the Gate and never reappeared at the destination Gate.

Most agree that the ship was simply lost with all hands due to an accident. Some though, the more superstitious types, think that the ship is stuck in the warp dimension, unable to come home. 

This even has led to any ship disappearance due to a Gate error to be labeled as a "[[Montauk Event]]". While the government and oversight bodies would lead the populace to believe that such events are rare, they are actually increasing in chance steadily for unknown reasons.
