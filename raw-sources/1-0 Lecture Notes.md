Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)
Financial information in the US is based on GAAP
Collection of accounting standards and assumptions that define how financial information will be reported
- Accounting standards are the rules that determine the accounting for individual business transactions
- Accounting principles and assumptions provide the framework upon which accounting standards are constructed

Within the US, the **Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB)** has the primary responsibility for developing standards

The **Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)**, an agency of the US govt, has autority over the accounting and financial disclosures for companies whose shares of ownership (stock) are traded and sold to the public

Outside the US, most countries use accounting standards and priciples adopted by the **International Accounting Standards Borad (IASB)**
