The party left the Soggy Alchemist and made their way north towards the North District to find [[Aronoc#Measured Knightstead|The Measured Knightstead]]. Instead what they found was quarantine and obstacles.

With some on the fly planning between the three, they were able to fool the guards into believing they had the proper paperwork necessary to make their way in to the district. Finding it completely devoid of life outside of the odd passing person or guards stationed around, they were almost alone in an otherwise bustling city.

Asking a nearby guard for directions, the party made their way to the school, where they met the friendly gnomish professor [[Aronoc#Zaffrab Symphony|Zaffrab Symphony]]. Zaffrab was able to concur they level of sorcery that had been cast on the trunk they confiscated, and with his considerable knowledge of magics and spells, was able to undo the locking spell.

Inside the group found nothing but dirt, dirt imbued with necromancy magic. Even Zaffrab was unable to determine the purpose without needing time to pour over his texts and notes. The party felt it was best to leave the trunk with Zaffrab to determine the reasoning, while they themselves would head into [[Aronoc#The Spires|The Spires]] to try and worm their way in to having an audience with [[Golgum]] himself. 

After a narrow dodge at being found out by the quarantine guards leaving the North District, the party made it to The Spires where they saw first hand the magnificence of the holy architecture. Finding the temple to [[Lathander]] was simple enough, and once inside the party was approached by a kindly old lizardfolk woman asking for donations to help stem a rash of Tiefling oppression in the city after an unfortunate bit of arson had caused the whole city to turn against the race. After donations and some clever wordplay, they were able to convince the priestess to try and ask about having an audience.

The party, beyond curious decided to send an invisible Arturas in after her with a quick invisibility spell from Petals. Alone aside from the spell message, Arturas found themselves eavesdropping on a conversation deep in the temple when the door was opened. In that instant all he could see was 4 priests in black cloaks....
