
**Thesis:** The ending of Home Movies ("Focus Grill") is **one of the best** endings in television. It is part of a larger series about shows with great endings. **Target runtime:** 30–34 minutes **Status:** Framework done. Sources listed below. Some items need a check before you script (see the last section).

---

## 1. Decisions from the committee

- Claim "one of the best", not "the best". This is a defense of the ending, not a ranking. Say so in the cold open.
- The camera breaks by **accident**. Brendon does not choose to give it up. Never write that he "chooses" or "rejects" the camera. The accident is the stronger reading: he does not need the camera any more, and he does not notice.
- Put the "great TV endings" comparison at the start and the end only. Do not put it in the middle, where it kills momentum before the emotional payoff.
- Act 3 (the close read of "Focus Grill") must be the longest act.

## 3. Sources used

These claims came from web searches. Source names are as the search results showed them. I did not keep the full URLs.

**Episode facts**

- "Focus Grill" is season 4, episode 13, and episode 52 overall. Brendon Small wrote it. Loren Bouchard directed it. It aired April 4, 2004 on Adult Swim. (Wikipedia episode page.)

**Camera scene**

- McGuirk helps Paula set up a grill. It fails in a burst of soot. The group goes to eat elsewhere. Brendon drops his camera while filming and a car crushes it. He tears up briefly, then a talk about where to eat pulls him back, and he forgets the camera. The last shot is the broken camera's view of an empty road, which glitches out. (Wikipedia episode page.)

**Finale plot**

- The kids find a tape of their unfinished first movie. They cannot agree on an ending, so each writes one and tests it on a focus group of classmates. Jason: gross-out monster attack. Melissa: her character's mother calls her home to a throne. Brendon: the trio lives in a house of trash. (Wikipedia episode page.)

**Creator intent**

- Small has said the finale was meant as an optimistic note. He has said the broken camera removes the character's crutch. He has said a later season would have shown Brendon branching out because the camera was gone. (Wikipedia and interview material cited there. Find the original interview before you quote him on screen.)

**Critical reception**

- A.V. Club retrospective ("It's very difficult to come up with an ending," but Home Movies nailed it). It argues that life continues after the screen goes dark. It also describes the finale as a run of small adjustments that build to one big event.
- Wikipedia notes a mostly positive response, with reviewers praising the sentimental, bittersweet close.

**Music cue**

- "Brendon's Camera" was used earlier in "Guitarmageddon" as the theme for Dwayne and Clarece. It is reused here for Brendon. (Wikipedia.)

**Season 4 build-up**

- Arts camp where the trio meets closed minds. School play collapses. They miss a local film festival. "Temporary Blindness": family-tree project that raises Melissa's worry about her absent mother, Brendon's worry about making his family look interesting, and Jason's invented pedigree for his ferret.
