As the party made their way closer to the [[Cragmaw Castle]], they heard goblinoid language as they made their way to the side entrance. 

As they made their way in they found themselves face to face with the Castles goblin cook and his assistants. A quick brawl ensued where the party swiftly dealt with the goblins before moving on. 

After clearing out most of the castle, room by room, the party came in contact with the leader of this particular goblin warband. A large, older goblin by the name of [[Cragmaw Castle#King Grol|King Grol]], who was in deep discussion at the time with a [[Vyerith|Drow woman]] who was demanding the turnover of a certain parchment that was in the possesion of [[Delrun#^371317|Gundren Rockseeker]] when kidnapped 2 days prior.

As the party came in, a battle royale ensued with everyone versus everyone. Eventually the Drow woman escaped as Grol was knocked unconscious. The party used the opportunity to inspect the room and found Gundren tied up and badly beaten in a nearby closet nook. 

Grol awoke to the party parlaying themselves into his good graces by abusing his low intellect and used his charisma with the goblinoids to amass forces from his warband to help take down The Black Spider.

The party used a room in the castle that had been occupied by Hobgoblins previously. As they spent the night they kept a careful eye on their surroundings while resting up and nursing their wounds.

In the morning the party made their way, with some assistance from a still shaken Gundren towards [[Wave Echo Cave]]. When they found the entance, the party laid eyes upon a torn up campsite in the first antechamber.....
