Direct Cost are identified with and can be traced to a cost object
- For example, the cost of wood to make guitars is a direct cost

Indirect Cost cannot be identified with  or traced to a cost object
- Ex. salaries of production managers cannot be traced to a single guitar

Manufacturing costs
- Cost of a manufactured good includes costs of making product as well as cost of converting materials
- Cost of a finished product includes
	- Direct Materials Cost
	- Direct Labor Cost
	- Factory Overhead Cost

Prime Cost = Direct Material + Direct Labor cost
Conversion Cost = Direct Labor + Factory Overhead

As product costs are incurred they are recorded on balance sheet as inventory. When sold, cost of manufactured product is reported as COGS on income statement

Period costs are expenses on income statement in the period where they are incurred, never show on balance sheet, only income statement

Three types of inventory for manufacturing business
- Materials or Raw Materials inventory
- Work in Process inventory
- Finished Goods inventory

Retail business determines COGS by adding net purchases for period to beginning inventory. Subtract end inventoy to determine COGS

Manu business makes the product and must calculate cost of goods manufactured, determined by preping a statement of cost of goods manufactured.
