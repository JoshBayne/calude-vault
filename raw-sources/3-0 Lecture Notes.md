# 3-0a Minimum Take Aways
1. Chapter 3 adds to vocabulary
2. GAAP Uses
	1. Accounting period concept (never longer than 12 months)
	2. Matching principle - revenue and expenses related to the same economic activity are reported in the same accounting period
3. Revenue recognition concept: Revenue is recognized when the revenue has been "earned"
4. A new revenue recognition standard changed how complex revenue recognition situations are treated for accounting purposes
5. Cash basis - use only for tax purposes
6. Adjusting entries assure that the financial statements comply with GAAP
7. Adjusting entried addresed in this chapter
	1. **Prepaid Expenses** (asset) - the amount prepaid will be recognized as an expense in the future
	2. **Accrued revenue** (asset) - revenue has been earned but not yet billed and recorded in accounts receivable
	3. **Unearned revenues** (liability) - the receipt of a pre-payment for a good or service to be delivered to the customer in the future
	4. **Accrued Expenses** (liability) - expenses that have been incurred but not yet paid
8. Fixed assets (may be called property, plant, and equipment or other similar terms) are called tangible assets used in the business that have a life that exceeds one year
9. Depreciations is the term used for expensing the cost of a fixed asset over the estimated useful life of that asset
10. Acumulated depreciation is the contra asset account that is credited each time a debit is made to depreciation expense
11. Book Value or net book value is Cost of the asset less the accumulated depreciation'
12. Vertical analysis - compares amoutns from the same year. Contrast this with horizontal analysis from Chapter 2
