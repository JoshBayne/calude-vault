[[Vampire adventure]]

Party watched the sermon of [[Disciples of Null#Bishop Vlodimire|Bishop Vlodimire]] after trying to speak to [[Golgum]]. After the sermon the party spoke more to Vlodimire and learned he was the second in command of the Lathander Cathedral. They made a deal with him to return the chest of dirt to Vlodimire on behalf of Golgum.

The party then decided to stay and watch closer to see if any of the priests or congregation members acted suspicious before attempting to scope out the cathedral after hours. What they found at first was simply priests having their dinner and going about their own business. It wasn't until they went to the same back room that Arturas encountered the 4 cloaked figures in earlier that day. Inside what they found was a badly wounded female priest laying in her own blood. 

Taegan attempted to heal her, before seeing that the wounds reopened almost immediately after the spell finished. Arturas, in an emotional fit decided to accost the innocent priests eating on the other side of that wing, showing them their wounded sister. They all agreed to help take the woman to [[Aronoc#Zaffrab Symphony|Zaffrab]] to see if he knew any magic that could help heal the poor woman. 

In the chaos of trying to get through the quarantine guards, the two priests helping were lost in the scuffle. The party however were able to make their way to Zaffrab where he attempted Regeneration on the girl. The spell worked at first before the wounds again began to reopen. The party then asked if he knew of a cleric who might be able to help, so he sent for [[Aronoc#Medrash Nommonis|Medrash Nommonis]] to come and try to help. While waiting for the dragonborn to arrive, the gnome showed the party a bone fragment he had found, where it was determined to be a section of a finger.

With some help from his god, and some esoteric knowledge, Medrash was able to determine that the poor soul was already too far gone and had began the process of turning into a vampire. After the party gave some final words, Petals dove one of her conjured Ice Knife deep into the chest of the nascent vampire. As she screamed her final breath, the vampire finally went dead. In a back alley they burned the body where Arturas, for some unknown reason, kept some of the ash.

It was then that the party decided that the plague sweeping the North District was vampiric in origin, and that Golgum was almost certainly the root cause. Before turning in for the night, Taegan and Arturas used their mage hands to sift through the cursed dirt to find more fragments.
