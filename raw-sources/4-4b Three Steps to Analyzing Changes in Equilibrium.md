### Three steps to analyzing changes in equilibrium
1. Decide whether the event shifts the supply curve, the demand curve, or, in some cases, both curves
2. Decide whether the curve shifts to the right or to the left
3. Use the supply-and-demand diagram
- Compare the initial and new equilibrium
- Effects on the equilibrium price and quantity

## Shift Vs Movement Along Curve
Change in Supply:
- A shift in the S curve
- Occurs when a non-price determinant of supply changes

Change in the quantity supplied:
- AA movement along a fixed S curve
- Occurs when P changes

Change in demand:
- A shift in the D curve
- Occurs when a non-price determinant of demand changes

Change in the quantity demanded:
- A movement along the fixed D curve
- Occurs when P changes
