
### Economic vs Accounting Profit

Profit = Total revenue - total cost
Total Revenue = Price x Quantity
Total Cost is the market value of all inputs a firm uses in production

Explicit Cost - input costs that require a firm to pay out money

Implicit Cost - input cost that do not require the payment of money like forgone wages or forgone interest on money in the bank

Economists: Total Cost = Explicit + Implicit costs
Accountants: Total Cost = Explicit costs

Economic Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost (Explicit and Implicit)
Accounting Profit = Total Revenue - Total Cost (explicit only)

Accounting profit usually larger

Total Cost (TC) = FC + VC

Average Total Cost (ATC) = TC/Q
Average Fixed Cost (AFC) = FC/Q
Average Variable Cost (AVC) = VC/Q
ATC=AFC+AVC

Marginal Cost - increase in total cost from adding a unit of production
Marginal Cost = Change in Total Cost / Change in Quantity = Delta TC/Delta Q

### Avg Total Cost in the Short Run and the Long Run
Short Run: 
fixed input

long run:
variable input

Economies of Scale - The property whereby long-run average total cost falls as the quantity of output increases
Constant Retruns to Scale - The property whereby long-run average total cost stays the same as the quantity of output changes
Diseconomies of Scale - The property whereby long-run average total cost rises as the quantity of output increases
