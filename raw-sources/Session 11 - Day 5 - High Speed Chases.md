Left [[Wave Echo Cave]] and made a deal with the surviving [[Delrun#Rockseeker Brothers|Rockseeker Brothers]] for 10% of any wealth generated from the mine once it is fully up and running in a year.
Elaineke left the party unexpectedly, while Ginny decided to stay in Delrun and help Sildar and the Rockseekers get the mine back up and running.

Met [[Delrun#Daran Edermath|Daran Edermath]] at his orchard and learned he was an adventurer who lost his memory due to the parties wizard wiping his mind.

Made their way north along the [[Kings Road]] on their way to [[Aronoc]]. 

As they made camp along the road, Arturas saw a strange cart roll past early in the morning before the sun rose that caused him to feel uncomfortable but curious. Arturas convinced the other party members to catch up to the cart and see what is inside. 

As they caught up to the cart, the party found a casket meant for [[Golgum]] with a magical lock, and Petals felt a deep and strong amount of necromancy magic inside the trunk. They also discovered that the driver for the cart was a zombie that collapsed into dust.

They decided to move on to Aronoc with the trunk and Arturas figured he could use his contacts in the city to try and learn more. They went over to [[Aronoc#Thin Pork|Thin Pork]], a bodega owned by [[Aronoc#Athena Luckytwist|Athena Luckytwist]] a kindly gnome who knows Arturas. She informed the party that Golgum is currently the High Priest of Lathander in The Spires. She also directed them over to [[Aronoc#The Soggy Alchemist|The Soggy Alhemist]] because [[Aronoc#Camerus Moor|Camerus Moor]] might know more about the trunk.

Camerus told the party that whatever kind of enchantments were far beyond his level of understanding and to try heading in to the New District at [[Aronoc#Measured Knightstead|The Measured Knightstead]] to learn more....
