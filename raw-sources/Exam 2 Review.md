## All the types of intellectual property, and the legal claims associated with each
**Patent** - Grant from the govt that gives inventor exclusive rights to invention. Lasts 20 yrs. Remedies for infrigement include monetary damages, incl royalties and attorney fees. Damages may be 3x for intentional infringement

**Copyright** - right of the author or originator or literary or artistic work, or other production that falls within a specific category, to have the exclusive use of that work for a given period. Automatic, only the expression of an idea can be protected. Author - lifetime of author plus 70 yrs. Publishers, 95yrs aftre publication date or 120 after creation. Remedies include actual damages plus profits received by infringing party or statutory damages u Copyright Act plus costs and attorneys fees

**Trademark** - Any distinctive word, name, symbol, or device (image, appearance) or combination thereof, that an entity uses to distinguish its goods or services from others. Owner has exclusive rights. At common law ownership created by use of mark. Registration with appropriate fed or state office gives notice and is permitted if the mark is currntly in use or will be within 6 months. Unlimited duration as long as in use. Must renew filing between 5th and 6th years and every 10 years after. Remedies include injusction to stop use, damages and profits, destruction of articles that infringed, costs and attorney fees

**Trade Secret** - any info a business has that gives advantage over others (formulas, lists, patterns, plans, etc). Granted through originality and development of the info and processes that constitute the secret and are unknown to others. Unlimited duration, so long as still secret. Remedies include monetary damages, costs and attorneys fees.

## Ways to terminate an offer
An offer may be terminated prior to acceptance by either 
- Action of the parties
	- revocation
		- offer can be withdrawn anytime before offeree accepts
		- offer is effective when the offeree or offeree's agent receives it
		- revocation may be accomplished by either
			- express repudiation of the offer
			- performance of acts inconsistent with the xistence of the offer and are made known to the offeree
	- rejection
		- by words or conduct
		- merely inquiring about an offer does not constitute rejection
		- effective only when it is received by the offeror or offeror's agent
	- counteroffer
		- Rejection of original offer and the simultaneous making of a new offer
- Operation of law
	- lapse of time
	- destruction of the specific subject matter of the offer
	- death or incompetence of the offeror or the offeree
	- supervening illegality of the proposed contract

## Cybersquatting
Occurs when a person registers a domain name that is the same as, or confusingly similar to, te trademark of another and then offers to sell the domain name back to the trademark owner.
ACPA, amended the Lanham act and make cybersquatting illegal when 
- Name is identical or confusingly similar to trademark of another
- There is bad faith intent to profit from trademark
Typosquatting - registering a name that is a misspell of a popular brand

## Comity
Legal reciprocity
One nation will defer and give effect to the laws and judicial decrees of another country, as long as the acts are consitent with the laws and public policy of the accommodating nation

## Act of State Doctrine
Judicial branch of one country will not examine the validity of public acts by foreign govt within its own territory

## Doctrine of Sovereign Immunity
Exempts foreign nations from jusrisdiction in US Courts
Codified in the Foreign sovereing immunities act (FSIA)
A foreign state is not immune from US Jurisdiction when:
- The foreign state has waived immunity
- Foreign state has engaged in commercial activity within the US or in commercial activity outside the US that has a "direct effect in the US"
- Foreign state has committed a tort in the US or in ciolation of certain international laws
- when a foreign state that has been designated "a state sponsor of terrorism" is sued under FISA for "personal injury or death that was caused by an act of torture" or related act of terrorism
