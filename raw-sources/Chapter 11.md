## Terms to Know
**Constituitive Rule of Communication** - Communication rule that tells us the meaning of a given message. Establshed by meaning. The understanding that communication is ever changing and ever evolving. For exampl, our social media reality shapes our communication. 
**Regulative Rule of Communication** - Communication rule that organizes communication to acknowledge the "who what when where and why" in relation to others. Examples include parents and grandparents only getting info from the news at specific times. The idea that there are only a handful of sources of "Absolute Communication"
**Slacktivism** - Concept defined by empty advocacy. All talk no action
