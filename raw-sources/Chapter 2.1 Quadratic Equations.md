A quadratic equation is an equation that can be written in the form ax^2+bx+c=0, where a does not equal 0 and a b and c are all real numbers. We often solve quadratic equations by factoring, based on the following property.
For real numbers a and b, if ab=0 then a=0 or b=0.
The most important thing to remember with solving by factoring is that one side of the equation must be zero. It does not work with any other number.

For example, we can solve the equation 6x^2=7x by factoring
6x^2=7x
6x^2-7x=0
x(6x-7)=0
x=0 or 6x-7=0
x=0 or x=7/6
