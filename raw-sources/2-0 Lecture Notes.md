#acct2301/chapter2 

Financial Statments prepared by a company must include
- Balance sheet
- income statement
- comprehensive income statement
- statement of changes in equity
- cash flow statement
*link to chapter 1*

Every account has a "normal" sign:
assets - "normal" sign debit
liabilities, equity - "normal" normal credit

Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Revenue - "normal" credit

Contra accounts have opposite normal sign for their category

Amounts opposite the normal sign are shown in (brackets) on a financial statement

Given assets are normal sign debit, a decrease to an asset account is a credit
