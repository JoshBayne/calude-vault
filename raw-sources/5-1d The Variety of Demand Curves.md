# The Variety of Demand Curves
Demand is considered 
	- *elastic* when elasticity is greater than one, which means the quantity moves proportionally more than the price. 
	- *inelastic* when elasticity is less than one, which means the quantity moves proportionally less than the price
If the elasticity is exactly one, the percentage change in quantity equals the percentage change in price, and demand is said to have *unit elasticity*

Because the price elasticity of demand measures how much quantity demanded responds to changes in the price, it is closely related to the slope of the demand curve. The flatter the demand curve passing through a given point, the greater the elasticity of demand. The steeper the demand curve passing through a given point, the smalled the price elasticity of demand. 

Inelastic curves look like the letter I.
