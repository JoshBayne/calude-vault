# Warp gates and Montauk Events

## Definition
In the sci-fi setting, **warp gates** are ancient structures of unknown origin that ships can use to jump between gates. A **Montauk Event** is any gate-error disappearance, named after the lost exploration ship UINSC Montauk — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]

## How it works / key claims
- The gates were discovered after the early-2020s JWST deployment, setting off a rush to chart the universe.
- The UINSC Montauk, a ship of the [[entities/united-interstellar-coalition]] more than 200 years old, vanished on its first jump; Montauk Events are quietly becoming more frequent.
- The Unitary Church of the Gates of God's Glory, founded right after the announcement, sees the gates as a road to God; its scholars sometimes take passage to study them. Others wonder what became of the builders.
- [[entities/dr-harrison-nguyen]] built systems to help map the gates, ending with the AI "AI-ex".
