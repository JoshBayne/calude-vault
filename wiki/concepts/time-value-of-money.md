# Time value of money

## Definition
A dollar now is worth more than a dollar later because it can earn a return; interest rates and inflation connect values across time.

## How it works / key claims
- **Rule of 72**: years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate (10% → about 7.2 years; check 1.1^7.2 ≈ 1.986).
- **Future value of an annuity**: $1,000 a year for 3 years at 10% = $3,310.
- **Real versus nominal**: real return ≈ nominal − inflation (8% − 3% = 5%), exactly (1.08 ÷ 1.03) − 1 = 4.85% by the Fisher equation.
- **Intrinsic value**: the sum of all expected future free cash flows converted into today's dollars. Value depends on the amount, timing and risk of the cash flows — [[sources/uhcl-syllabi-and-course-files]]
