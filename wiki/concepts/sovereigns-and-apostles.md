# Sovereigns and Apostles

## Definition
The cosmology behind the fantasy campaign's villains. **Sovereigns** are powerful entities outside destiny, incarnations of negative concepts and emotions, who cannot be killed by normal means. **Apostles** are beings who made a deal with them to exist outside causality — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]], [[topics/arian-and-belotria-gazetteer]]

## How it works / key claims
- Known Apostles: [[entities/nezznar-the-black-spider]] (restarting the Wave Echo Cave mine to forge weapons) and [[entities/golgum]] (man-bat whose venom makes vampire slaves).
- Each Apostle works through servants: the Redbrands for Nezznar, the [[entities/disciples-of-null]] for Golgum, who is said to be taking Aronoc "for the Sovereigns".
- Null, "sovereign of darkness and emptiness", is explained by the Religious Library's keeper in the `Vampire adventure` notes — [[entities/disciples-of-null]]

## Related concepts
- [[entities/wave-echo-cave]] — where the first Apostle was fought

## Open questions and disagreements
- Whether Nezznar and Golgum coordinated is not stated.
- Whether Null is one of the Sovereigns is implied, not stated.
- The notes name no other Sovereigns or Apostles.
