# TikTok safe zones

## Definition
The area of a 1080×1920 (9:16) video where text, faces and logos stay visible once TikTok's interface is drawn on top. Content outside it can be hidden by the username band, the icon rail or the caption and CTA area.

## How it works / key claims
- Overlays, as one vendor guide gives them: top about 150–200 px, right edge about 120 px, bottom about 250–300 px. The remaining center band is the safe zone — [[sources/tiktok-safe-zones-guide-zeely]]
- The overlay changes by placement. In-Feed lifts the CTA as captions grow; Spark Ads show the username sooner; TopView adds a playback bar; LIVE and Shopping crowd the bottom third — [[sources/tiktok-safe-zones-guide-zeely]]
- Practical rules: bold, high-contrast type; preview in TikTok's own tools; test on two phones; run A/B tests on caption and CTA position.
