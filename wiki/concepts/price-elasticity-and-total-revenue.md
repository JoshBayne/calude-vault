# Price elasticity and total revenue

## Definition
Price elasticity of demand = %ΔQd ÷ %ΔP (midpoint method). Demand is elastic above 1, inelastic below 1, unit elastic at 1.

## How it works / key claims
- Determinants: close substitutes, necessity vs luxury, how narrowly the market is defined, time horizon (more elastic over time).
- Total revenue test: with inelastic demand price and revenue move together; with elastic demand they move opposite; at unit elasticity revenue is unchanged.
- Along a linear demand curve elasticity falls as price falls: elastic at high prices, 1 at the midpoint (revenue peak), inelastic at low prices. The notes' table peaks at revenue 24 between prices 4 and 3 (recomputed).
- Supply elasticity grows with time; milk example: output 9,000 → 11,000 gallons as price rises $2.85 → $3.15 gives 20% ÷ 10% = 2 (recomputed).
- Price discrimination (monopoly chapter) charges different buyers prices based on willingness to pay: movie discounts, coupons, quantity discounts.
