# D&D Campaigns and Worldbuilding

A large cluster (roughly 100 files) of tabletop-RPG material: a running fantasy campaign built on *Lost Mine of Phandelver* and a separate sci-fi setting. Speech-topic overlap: [[topics/public-speaking]] (TTRPGs listed as an "advocacy" topic). Other creative projects: [[topics/personal-and-hobbies]].

## 1. Fantasy campaign: "Lost Mines of Delrun"

`LMoP` records that the published adventure (*Lost Mine of Phandelver*, linked from 5e.tools) was **"retooled as Lost Mines of Delrun"**, the party's first adventure, taking them from **levels 1 to 5**. Setting tags place it in the country of **Arian** in the world of **Belotria**.

### The party
Current: **Petals Lillydew**, **Taegan Sungem**, **Arturas Azzakod**. Former: **Elaineki St. Germain** (left unexpectedly after session 10) and **Ginevieve "Ginny" Hallowisp** (stayed in Delrun to help restart the mine).

### Places, factions and NPCs (from file names and notes read)
Full gazetteer, factions and planning notes: [[topics/arian-and-belotria-gazetteer]].
- **Delrun**: frontier town in the Emerald Wood, rebuilt over an ancient city (pre-dates the **Delrundian Pact**); no Abiding Hand protection; locations include Townmaster's Hall, Stonehill Inn (Toblen Stonehill), Barthen's Provisions, Lionshield Coster (Linene Graywind), Edermath Orchard (Daran Edermath, a retired adventurer whose memory was wiped by a wizard), and Tresendar Manor (purchasable by the party). The notes also record that Linene is on leave in the **Port of Biscayne** after an incident with the party.
- **Rockseeker brothers**: Gundren (rescued), Tharden (killed by Nezznar), Nundro (rescued).
- **Disciples of Null**: religious order, vampires made by **Golgum** to take Aronoc for **The Sovereigns**; leaders Bishop Vlodimire, Sister Nora, Father Ubik, Father Conrad. A homebrew **Disciple of Null** stat block (CR 1, undead, mixes zombie "Loathsome Limbs" with vampire regeneration, spider climb and a 3/day enslave) is in the notes.
- The linked setting pages (Aronoc, Vindun, Kings Road, Lathander, The Harpers, Yartar, Lionshields, Apostles, Sovereigns, Nothic, Venomfang, Cragmaw Castle/Hideout, Thundertree, Wave Echo Cave, Redbrand, Possible Adventure Hooks) are now summarized in [[topics/arian-and-belotria-gazetteer]]; `_Monsters` and `_Initiative Tracker` remain unread.

## 2. Sci-fi setting (United Interstellar Coalition)
Expanded lore, characters and story beats: [[topics/sci-fi-setting-uic]].
- **Warp Gates**: discovered after the JWST deployment in the early 2020s; unknown origin but usable to warp ships between gates. The **Unitary Church of the Gates of God's Glory** sees them as a road to God; others wonder what happened to the gates' builders.
- **UINSC Montauk**: a >200-year-old exploration ship of the **UIC** that vanished on its first jump into a Gate; any gate-error disappearance is now called a **"Montauk Event"**, and these are quietly becoming more frequent.
- **Locations**: the **Crimson World** (original home of the **Dogu**, a planet-wide megacity scorched by a failing atmosphere; a critique of unchecked profit-seeking), **Chobo** (home of Soren; where Gat grew up), and a small converted courier-turned-pirate **ship** nicknamed after *Going Merry*.
- **Desert Between Worlds**: a sand sea between planes with black stars, with a sky modeled on a JoJo Part 3 fight.
- Other sci-fi pages (`Earth`, `Net`, `The Clan`, `Dr. Harrison Nguyen` and more) were read later; see [[topics/sci-fi-setting-uic]] for the verified pages and the ink-only files. (An earlier version of this line listed `Level-List`, which is a guitar roadmap — see [[topics/personal-and-hobbies]] — and `TVOM(2)`, a link-only PDF of unknown subject.)
