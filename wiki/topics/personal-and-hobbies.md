## Guitar practice (`Guitar-Chord-Changes-Practice`, Aug–Sep 2024)
- Tracks clean chord changes per minute. Goal: **30/min initially, 60/min eventually**; a strum only counts if every note rings clean.
- Early scores were about 17–22/min (A–D, D–E, A–E, 8-13). By early September they reached the 30s (best logged: 39 for A–D on 9-4, 39 for D–E on 9-6). A second table adds Am–E, Em–D, Am–Em (best 50 for Am–Em on 9-4).
- Technique notes: stop muting the high E string, practice with a metronome, first week of forced pick use.
- Routine follows Justin Guitar's beginner course: tuning, Am/Em chord practice, one-minute changes, anchor fingers, strumming, "Seven Nation Army" riff, and module-3 songs; a Hound Dog progression is noted for electric.

## Guitar learning roadmap (`Level-List`, Sept 2025)
A checklist of skill levels 0–6. **Level 0** (all checked): hold guitar and pick, parts of the guitar, basic strumming, single notes, string names, tuning, notes/chords/keys, half vs whole steps. **Level 1**: open chords E, Em, A, Am, G, C, D (checked) and changing strings (checked); still open: strumming in time and learning starter songs (e.g. "For What It's Worth", "Wagon Wheel", "Smoke on the Water", "Seven Nation Army", "Iron Man"). **Level 2**: alternate picking, first-position minor pentatonic, fretboard notes to the 5th fret, syncopation. **Level 3**: major pentatonic, barre chords, all fretboard notes, soloing over backing tracks. **Level 4**: all five pentatonic positions, diatonic scales, seventh chords, bends/vibrato/hammer-ons, harmonics, scale and triad theory (a teacher is commonly recommended here). **Level 4.5**: harmonizing scales, the Nashville Number System, common progressions. **Level 5**: modes, intervals, the CAGED system, arpeggios. **Level 6**: chord extensions, melodic and harmonic minor. This matches the practice log above, where open-chord changes are the current focus.
