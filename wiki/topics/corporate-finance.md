## What the notes actually contain

### Commercial lending (`Loan-Structuring-Presentation`, Oct 2023)
- **C&I** = commercial and industrial lending; **EBITDA** is the profit measure commonly used by commercial bankers.
- Loan types: **lines of credit** (can be seasonal, though usually not) and **term loans**.
- Investment banks above a certain size stop holding balance sheets and focus almost entirely on raising capital.
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)** = (EBITDA − taxes − distributions − maintenance cost) ÷ (interest + principal).

### Personal financial planning lecture (`Chapter-2`, FINC 3301, June 2023)
Slide text from Billingsley/Gitman/Joehnk, *Personal Financial Planning* 15e:
- **50/30/20 rule**: 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, 20% to financial goals (saving, investing, emergency fund, debt reduction).
- **Statements**: a **balance sheet** reports assets, liabilities and net worth at a date; an **income and expense statement** (cash basis) reports a period; budgets are forward-looking. **Net worth = assets − liabilities**; assets are measured at fair market value (what you could sell them for).
- **Ratios**: savings ratio = cash surplus ÷ net income; debt service ratio = monthly loan payments ÷ monthly gross income; solvency ratio = net worth ÷ total assets; liquidity ratio = liquid assets ÷ current debts. (The slides ask whether given moves, e.g. savings 15%→20% or debt service 15%→30%, are good or bad.)
- **Pessimistic budgeting works**: people underestimate short-term expenses, so deliberately pessimistic short-term budgets are more accurate.
- **Time value of money**: **Rule of 72** (years to double ≈ 72 ÷ rate; $1,000 at 10% doubles in about 7.2 years, check: 1.1^7.2 ≈ 1.986); **future value of an annuity** ($1,000 a year for 3 years at 10% = **$3,310**).
- **Inflation**: real return ≈ nominal − inflation (8% − 3% = 5%), but the **Fisher equation** is exact: (1.08 ÷ 1.03) − 1 = **4.85%**.
