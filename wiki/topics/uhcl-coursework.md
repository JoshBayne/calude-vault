## Notes not tied to a course page
- **Introduction to Business** (`Chapter 2 - Thriving in Business`): **emotional intelligence** is the ability to recognize and understand emotions in yourself and others and use that awareness to manage behavior and relationships; four domains: self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship management.
- **Operations** (`Operations`): services vs products. Product quality can be tested before the customer uses it, service quality is judged after delivery; capacity for products can be built ahead of demand, service capacity scales with the number of providers; services use less capital and automation.
- **Medieval history** (Jan 2024): `Week-2` lists readings on grave goods in early medieval burials (mandatory), *Tamed Death* and embalming/dissecting the corpse (optional), with pre-class questions on why people venerated saints, whether medieval medicine helped ordinary people, and why bodies were embalmed. `Primary-Source-Pack` and `Papers` are empty.
- `Cap Paper`, a short outline for a "how my thinking changed" paper on reality TV and musicals, is probably the COMM 1315 communication analysis paper, which the syllabus calls the "CAP PAPER" (inference; see [[topics/public-speaking]]).
- Exam instructions: `Lockdown Browser UHCL` covers LockDown Browser and webcam rules for Blackboard exams, with no course named — [[sources/uhcl-syllabi-and-course-files]]
- `Untitled-page` and `Syllabus-Day-Notes` (image dumps) have no text.

## Cross-course links
- Opportunity cost and marginal analysis ([[topics/economics]]) reappear in differential analysis ([[topics/managerial-accounting]]) and in competitive-firm profit maximization (MR = MC).
- Linear functions and break-even ([[topics/math-1324-business-math]]) match the Bagels R Us break-even in [[topics/statistics]] and the profit functions in [[topics/managerial-accounting]].
- Externalities and public goods in [[topics/economics]] extend into [[topics/energy-and-environmental-economics]].
- Inventory valuation in [[topics/intermediate-accounting]] builds on [[topics/financial-accounting]]; the personal-finance time-value formulas in [[topics/corporate-finance]] preview Intermediate Accounting Chapter 5.

## Caveat
Many files are OneNote exports consisting of slide images or links to PowerPoints; where only a title survives, the source table on each page says so. Syllabi record what each course planned to cover ([[sources/uhcl-syllabi-and-course-files]]), not what the author studied. FINC 3331 is the stated prerequisite for both FINC 4331 and 4351, but the vault has no files for it. The syllabi for ECON 2302, ACCT 2301 and MATH 1324 are scans and are still unread.
