# Cross-domain connections

## Current synthesis
The notes look like separate silos (school, a small business, tabletop games, a journal), but the same handful of ideas recur: choosing under scarcity, pricing and revenue, checking numbers, and organized economic power. Each link below is marked **stated** (the notes say it) or **inference** (my reading, worth checking).

## Major themes
- **One idea, four disguises: opportunity cost.** The PPF and comparative advantage in economics, the $125–$180 transfer-price range in a managerial-accounting exercise, "regret" in decision analysis, and the 50/30/20 budget are the same trade-off. *Inference* that regret is opportunity cost under uncertainty. — [[concepts/opportunity-cost]], [[concepts/decision-analysis-and-regret]]
- **Break-even appears in four places.** Linear profit functions (math), "Bagels R Us" (quantitative analysis), shutdown rules and overhead rates (economics/accounting), and a debt-service coverage ratio of 1.0 as break-even for loans (*inference*). — [[concepts/break-even-and-profit-functions]]
- **The checking technique found an error in the notes themselves.** The trial-balance lesson says a difference divisible by 9 signals a transposition; the NetSolutions credits are off by 90, and the same method flags Fees Earned 16,430 vs 16,340. Recomputation also found an inconsistent fixed cost (3600 vs 36000) in a math break-even example. *Inference* on the exact digit swap. — [[concepts/error-detection-and-controls]]
- **Law as applied economics.** Patents and copyrights are legally granted monopolies (*inference*); the criminal vs civil burden of proof resembles choosing a significance level (*inference*); spam and typosquatting impose costs on shared communication channels (*inference*). — [[concepts/market-power-and-cartels]], [[concepts/externalities-and-public-goods]], [[concepts/statistical-inference]]
- **Fiction as a laboratory for economics.** The Clan's protection pact vs unstable cartels, the Delrundian Pact as a joint venture, the UIC as a bloated monopoly of governance, and the Redbrands as an extraction racket serving the Black Spider. *All inference;* the fiction pages never use these terms. — [[entities/the-clan]], [[entities/united-interstellar-coalition]], [[entities/nezznar-the-black-spider]]
- **Pricing theory meets a real project.** Elasticity and price discrimination in the coursework line up with a requested tiered-pricing calculator for the princess-party business. *Inference;* the pricing transcript was only skimmed. — [[concepts/price-elasticity-and-total-revenue]], [[entities/sophias-magical-princess-party]]
- **A story-level link between the two fantasy antagonists.** Nezznar and Golgum are both Apostles of the Sovereigns, and both operate through servants (Redbrands; Disciples of Null), but the notes never say they coordinated. — [[entities/golgum]], [[entities/nezznar-the-black-spider]], [[entities/disciples-of-null]]
- **Hobbies feed coursework.** The Speech 1 topic list names TTRPGs among advocacy ideas (*stated*), tying the public-speaking course to the campaign. — [[topics/public-speaking]], [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]]
