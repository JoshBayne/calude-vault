# Information Systems and Security

Management information systems (MIS) concepts and the beginnings of a security module. Technical network foundations are in [[topics/networking]]; business-strategy framing connects to [[topics/corporate-finance]] and [[topics/uhcl-coursework]].

## Core concepts

### Module 1: What is an information system?
- An **information system** is a set of interrelated components supporting operations, reporting, analysis, decision making and coordination.
- **Competitive advantage** via cost leadership, differentiation, or focus.
- **Leavitt's Diamond**: people, technology infrastructure, process, structure. People are the most important element. A *process* adds value to inputs; a *procedure* is a set of steps.
- **Types**: personal, workgroup (collaboration), enterprise, interorganizational.
- **Value chain / supply chain**: primary processes (inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, service) plus support processes (technology infrastructure, HR, accounting and finance, procurement).
- **Strategic planning** gives direction, focuses resources, enables proactivity and improves communication. The **IS strategic planning spectrum** runs from cost center/service provider (inward-looking) to business partner to game changer (outward-looking, innovation).
- **Careers/roles**: CIO, software developer, security analyst, systems analyst, programmer, web developer, business analyst; certifications as continuing education.
- **Shadow IT**: systems built outside the IS department. Pros: speed, innovation, testing many ideas. Cons: weak security/compliance, tension with the CIO, duplication, unclear support responsibility.
