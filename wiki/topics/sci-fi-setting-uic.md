# Sci-Fi Setting: The United Interstellar Coalition

A separate campaign/story setting from the fantasy world in [[topics/arian-and-belotria-gazetteer]]. Most pages are dated **May 2022** (many are handwritten ink drawings with no extractable text). Overview and the Warp Gates / UINSC Montauk lore are on [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]].

## Premise
- **Earth** (`Earth`): space travel has become routine. The early-2020s **JWST** launch led to the discovery of ancient **warp gates**, triggering a rush to chart the universe and a new age of exploration where people seek glory in the uncharted. See `Warp Gates` and `UINSC Montauk` on the D&D page.
- **UIC** (`UIC`): born from the ashes of the old Earth United Nations and the main governing body of humanity and its worlds for about **five hundred years**. Ostensibly for everyone's benefit, it has become a bloated bureaucracy unable to stop corruption among its upper ranks. Headquartered on **Mars**, it keeps peace through its **Navy** and is led by the **Council of 13**, who use **Headhunters** as a private peacekeeping force.
- **The Clan** (`The Clan`): a network of affiliated gangs and mafias across the human empire. Each cell works independently and does not interfere with others' business or territory; membership also means a **protection pact**: when one group issues an APB, all must honor it.
- **The Net** (`Net`): the future internet, browsed from a terminal or fully immersive with goggles and gloves as an endless virtual city.
- **Nambase** (`Nambase`): a solar-punk world originally settled by people from places like Nairobi and Lagos; every building doubles as a greenhouse, so large dense cities avoid pollution.
- **Unitary Church of the Gates of God's Glory**: founded right after the gates were announced; members believe they are a road to God, and church scholars sometimes take passage on ships to study them.
- **Other locations**: Mars, Chobo and Dogu (the Crimson World) exist as ink-only pages; see the D&D page for their descriptions from `Locations`.

## Characters and story (as far as the text goes)
- **Dr. Harrison Nguyen**: a kind, honest government researcher on Chobo who built systems to help map the gates, culminating in the AI **AI-ex** (the file `AI-ex` is ink only). He came to see the AI as a daughter; when ordered to hand it to the government he chose to run, using **Steph** to get forged papers for another world's visa terminals and a captain who rents out a ship. He now depends on **Gat** to get him safely off-world.
- **Story beat ideas**: open with a flashback of a boy told the stars could be his; the protagonist is a driven future space captain working at a motel near a starport (a Gattaca-style figure who studies piloting in the downtime). One night a cagey man arrives with a young child; when the protagonist threatens to call authorities over suspected trafficking, the man reveals he is a government researcher disgusted that the government uses AIs as slave labor.
- **Scenes**: *First Dinner*: Alex cooks for the ship but, using only the most literal definitions of foods and techniques, produces technically immaculate but wrong-tasting food. *First Flight*: Gat flies perfectly by the book; the captain says flying is as much art as science, and Gat asks to be taught.
