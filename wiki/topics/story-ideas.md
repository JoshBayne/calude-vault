# Story ideas

## Current synthesis
Seven short fiction notes from the author's old phone notes, none finished: two unfinished scenes, four premises and one hook line. All came in one batch, [[sources/phone-old-notes-batch-2026-10-31]]. Writing dates are unknown. Most lean toward cosmic or sci-fi dread. None names a setting, so any link to the author's existing worlds below is inference.

## Major themes
- **Structural integrity failing** (about 500 words, unfinished) — a lone pilot flies a failing craft deep into an anomaly to release a payload, singing to himself while the crew on the main vessel listen. The craft breaks up and the ship's computer reports being in hundreds of places at once. He falls for what feels like months, his oxygen barely used, then "Space came rushing back. The stars didn't match."
- **Cult** (about 450 words, unfinished) — a first-person narrator watches black-hooded figures gather below the apartment every night, one to fifteen at a time. One night four of them look up. Next day a letter in the door, addressed to the narrator's full legal name and on strange creaseless paper, apologises for the "proceedings" and calls them harmless.
- **Fathers Love** — a long-lost father, perhaps a pirate or a member of some organization, has his soul bound to a ship. His son has done everything to find his family. The father goes out "in a blaze of glory" to protect him.
- **King in Crimson** — a city covering a whole planet, gold and crimson buildings, a sky in perpetual golden hour, and a god-king in gold armor and crimson robes.
- **Dark Gods Dream** — manifestations of humanity's darkest traits (greed, rage, lust) spread through a thriving metropolis like a disease. One person in a white robe flees, then returns for fear of losing loved ones. The filename suggests it came from a dream (inference).
- **Way Gates** — relics of a lost galactic power that open a "massive webway of jump points". It asks why they were turned off, what happened to their creators and whether something else came through.
- **Story hook** (`story hook ikea`) — "The stars scream. Did you know that?" The "ikea" in the filename is unexplained.

## Points of agreement
Recurring elements, by inference: an unknowable space threat (*Structural integrity failing*, *Way Gates*, the hook), a single person facing it alone, and sacrifice for loved ones (*Structural integrity failing*, *Fathers Love*, *Dark Gods Dream*).

## Tensions and contradictions
None; there is one source.

## Implications
Possible links to the author's sci-fi setting, all inference:
- *Way Gates* closely matches [[concepts/warp-gates-and-montauk-events]]: ancient gates, unknown builders, and things going wrong. Whether it is an early draft of that lore or a separate idea is unknown.
- *King in Crimson* resembles the Crimson World, also a planet-wide megacity, on [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]]. The tone differs, since that world is scorched and critiques profit-seeking. The ink-only page `Crimson-Empire-Invades` has no readable text to compare.
- *Structural integrity failing* could fit a Montauk Event, since the pilot ends up where the stars don't match.
