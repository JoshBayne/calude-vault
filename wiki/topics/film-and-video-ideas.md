# Film and video ideas

## Current synthesis
Three note fragments pushed from the phone on 2026-10-31 (writing dates unknown) sketch short video or film premises, two of them built around [[entities/sophia-bayne]] and her TikTok and princess-costume work. They are idea notes only; nothing says any was shot, and the notes are unsigned beyond the author's vault. A second batch of older phone notes adds video-essay titles, Star Trek sketch ideas and meme premises. Fiction premises are on [[topics/story-ideas]]. Essays with finished plans are on [[topics/video-essay-series]].

## Major themes
- **The Doll 2 (a "rebootquel")** — a sequel to a film called *The Doll*, which the note calls "canonically a family movie". The wiki has no other record of the original — [[sources/phone-inbox-notes-2026-10-31]]
  - Setup: Gwen is still afraid of the doll; Sophia thinks it was all a gag; the doll turns out to be really evil, or a jokester. Most scares have a rational explanation until "the doll does her big one". The parents still live in the haunted house, Sophia and Josh are moving and taking the doll, and Gwen has moved across the country. A carbon monoxide leak that kills the parents is floated as the way Sophia finds the doll again.
  - Style notes: reference the original enough to let viewers in on the joke; reuse original footage in a scene of Sophia studying the first film; the woman the doll came from as a child was spooky.
  - Doll antics list: ruining food, messing with cars, breaking the fridge, thumps that startle the dogs, hiding phone, remote and keys, messing with the computer, and "murder?". Last line: the doll is a witch herself and needs a human's help.
- **Princess Traffic** — opens on Sophia walking to a house in full princess costume. She knocks, steps in, and a bag is thrown over her head as the door slams. A quick cut to an obvious time jump shows another girl in princess costume approaching the same door. It reads as a dark-comedy or horror short (inference from the three beats; no title meaning or ending is given).
- **TikTok Live** — a live commenter gets "a little too personal"; Sophia ends the live and then someone knocks at the door. Echoes her real TikTok live shows ([[sources/journal-2026-10-29]]) and the door-knock beat of *Princess Traffic* (inference).
- **Video-essay titles** (`YouTube video Ideas`) — verdicts on endings and adaptations — [[sources/phone-old-notes-batch-2026-10-31]]
  - Good endings: *Seinfeld*, *Home Movies*, the *Ed, Edd n Eddy* movie as "the perfect send off".
  - Things that fail: *Heathers the Musical* ("doesn't work"), *If/Then*, *RENT* ("Why I hate RENT"), *Beetlejuice Beetlejuice*.
  - Superheroes: why Green Lantern is the author's favorite, and "Why is it so hard to 'get' Superman?"
  - *Seinfeld*, *Home Movies*, *Ed, Edd n Eddy* and *RENT* now have frameworks. *Dear Evan Hansen*, which is not on this list, has a short one inside its summary — [[topics/video-essay-series]]
- **Star Trek sketches** (`star trek YouTube videos`) — Worf never gets listened to, the range of Troi's empathy, Wesley at school, Barclay calling out La Forge for holodeck shenanigans, and more holodeck shenanigans. Plan: find one thing in each episode to spoof — [[sources/phone-old-notes-batch-2026-10-31]]
- **Meme ideas** — a demon cat doing the "don't go" cat meme; the Bat signal showing 👌 with "RIP"; Jafar covering his tracks on a massive facility while a finance advisor in *Aladdin* asks where the money is going; a *Twilight* joke about the werewolf Bella punched — [[sources/phone-old-notes-batch-2026-10-31]]
