# Website builder project

## Current synthesis
On or before 2026-11-06 the owner began building a website builder by "vibe coding" (building with an AI assistant). He uses Sophia's website as the starting codebase. His goal is that, as the product evolves, none of her site's code remains in it. He plans to sell it, but has not decided how to stop buyers from passing the files on — [[sources/journal-2026-11-06]]

## Major themes
- **Origin in Sophia's site.** Five days earlier he had used Claude to make "her website" "slightly more efficient" — [[sources/journal-2026-11-01]]. That her website is the princess-party site in [[topics/princess-party-business-smpp]] is probable but not stated.
- **Clean separation.** The stated aim is that no code from her site remains. The notes do not say how this will be checked.
- **Selling and copy protection.** Sophia raised the risk that buyers hand the files to other people. The two options he names are an executable with a license key, or encryption of some kind. Research is deferred until release is closer — [[sources/journal-2026-11-06]]

## Points of agreement
The 11-01 and 11-06 notes agree that Claude is the tool in use for work on her site.

## Tensions and contradictions
None yet.

## Implications
Maintainer's interpretation, not from the notes:
- The way the product is delivered decides the copy problem. Files given to a buyer can be copied; encryption of website code that a browser must run gives little protection. A hosted service, where buyers sign in and never get the files, avoids most of the problem but adds hosting work. A license-key app sits in between. These options were not researched for this page.
- If the builder is to be sold, the templates must not use the studio-character names flagged on [[topics/princess-party-business-smpp]] (the `lightbox` character page).

## Open questions
- Product name, tech stack, where the code is kept, and the target buyers.
- Whether the starting site is the SMPP site.
- Which delivery model (files, licensed app or hosted service) he chooses.
