# Arian and Belotria Gazetteer

Setting reference for the fantasy campaign summarized in [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]] ("Lost Mines of Delrun"). The sci-fi setting is a separate world: [[topics/sci-fi-setting-uic]]. Notes are tagged `#belotria/country/arian/...` in the source files.

## Country and politics
- **Arian**: a principality on the north-western part of the continent of **Belotria**, ruled by **Regent Prince Balan** because **King Aldric** has been in an unending, unwakeable sleep for about a year (heartbeat and breathing only; the cause is an "unknown affliction"). Balan has few trustworthy advisors and fears he is not ready to rule.
- **The Abiding Hand**: Arian's main law enforcement.
- **Bentarik**: an organization trying to covertly control northern Arian.
- **Neighbors**: **Edenrath** (country to the east, containing **Yartar**, home of the merchant company **The Lionshields**) and **Kranghorn**, an independent dwarven city-state inside Arian's eastern edge.

## Cities and towns
- **Allantine**: capital of Arian.
- **Aronoc, "City of Lights"** (coastal): five districts.
  - *Dwarven district*: fabrication and manufacturing.
  - *Trade district*: the main marketplace.
  - *North district*: seat of power; elders' council (Lord Hightower listed); the **Measured Knightstead** magic school, whose only active teacher is the gnome **Zaffrab Symphony** (frizzy-haired, inquisitive, newly into alchemy).
  - *Old District*: poorer and rundown, with many orphans and transients; **The Soggy Alchemist** (shop with an illicit back room, run by the pale wizard **Camerus Moor**) and **Thin Pork** (bodega; **Athena Luckytwist**, a former parental figure to Arturas until he learned of her organized-crime ties).
  - *The Spires*: temples of **Eldath, Helm and Lathander**; the Religious Library is kept by **Medrash Nommonis**, a dragonborn cleric of Helm with encyclopedic knowledge of esoteric religions.
- **Eingrass** (west coast): famous for a huge bazaar. **Port of Biscayne**: the shipping hub, run by Chief Mornar **Ismuth Tidestride** (half-orc). **Walled City of Amrhan** (eastern border): Haunted Gate, Trade Town, Mist Gate, Castle Olgrach (seat of **Duke Bramrir**) and the Priests Lawn park. **Vindun**: magic college with a large elf and gnome population. **Loth'Loric Estates**: luxurious elf/half-elf community south of Eingrass (Ginny's home). **Eastfell**: halfling farming town.
- **Kings Road**: Arian's main road, linking Allantine, Aronoc, Eingrass, Port of Biscayne, Amrhan and Vindun; generally safe but slow.

## Landmarks and history
- **Emerald Wood** (50 miles northwest of Delrun), **Everflow River** (runs down the **Godpeak Mountains**, through the wood to the sea), **Godpeak Mountains** (divide a section of Arian).
- **Delrundian Pact**: an old agreement among dwarves, gnomes and humans of the Delrun area to jointly operate a mine at **Wave Echo Cave** with equal access to its **Forge of Spells**.
- **Wave Echo Cave**: fifteen miles east of Delrun in the Sword Mountains; the lost mine of the Phandelver's Pact (the published adventure's name; the campaign calls the agreement the Delrundian Pact). The source file is long text from the published adventure (a 20-area dungeon with wandering-monster tables, traps, undead guardians and the villain's lair), so it is not summarized in detail here. In the campaign it is where the party faces Nezznar.
- **Cragmaw Hideout** and **Cragmaw Castle** (ruined castle in the Emerald Wood; King Grol's base): cleared by the party. **Thundertree**: ruin from an orc invasion decades ago; home of the druid **Reidoth**. **Tresendar Manor**: ancient manor outside Delrun. See [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]] for the session recap.

## Factions and figures
- **Sovereigns**: powerful entities outside destiny, incarnations of negative concepts and emotions, who cannot be killed by normal means. **Apostles**: beings who made deals with them to exist outside causality; examples are **Nezznar the Black Spider** (a drow restarting the Wave Echo Cave mine to forge weapons) and **Golgum**.
- **Golgum**: an Apostle who looks like a suave young elf with amber eyes; his true form is a man-bat whose venom makes vampire slaves (who in turn make zombies). He poses as a priest in Lathander's temple in Aronoc, feeding on the ill and infirm, plotting to enslave the city.
- **Vyerith**: a doppelganger loyal to the Black Spider; escaped with the Wave Echo Cave map from Cragmaw Castle, reappeared in the cave leading bugbears, and remains at large.
- **Redbrand**: a small gang founded by **Glasstaff** to find slave labor for the Black Spider; defeated and dissolved by the party. **Gary**: a young, dumb human who wants to belong; found with a dragon cult. **Venomfang**: a young green dragon last seen leaving Thundertree with her egg. **Nothic**: one has a **blood pact** with Taegan that it will collect on.
- **The Harpers**: network of adventurers and spies who covertly oppose abuse of power and aid the oppressed. **Lathander**: god of renewal, rebirth and the dawn (priests wear white). **Decahnul Order**: people compelled to help those in need. **The Grove**: a fairy kingdom ruled by Queen Chrysanthemos. **Horses**: named Star and War.
- **Disciples of Null**: see [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]].

## Planning notes
- **`Vampire adventure`**: scene prep for the Spires. Struck-through text (resolved scenes) covers Disciples of Null sensing the invisible Arturas, Golgum refusing audiences except via the Council (referring the party to Bishop Vlodimire or Sister Nora), a zero-sum bargain over cursed earth, and charm/dominate combat. Live text: if the party beats the four disciples and shows their symbol to Medrash, he explains a dark cult of **Null, sovereign of darkness and emptiness**, and that the casket carvings are depictions of the last "**Ceremony of the Eclipse**"; guards are now hunting the party after they were seen carrying a body at night.
- **`Possible Advnture Hooks`** (sic): Aronoc is powered by a lead-acid battery whose "acid" is a giant gelatinous cube; an unmanned ship in Port of Biscayne; a potion-maker who really wants the party's bodies; a lord whose home Arturas burned seeks revenge; nobles want to silence Taegan; an escalating underground fight club (Ginny); trouble at a fairy hollow (Petals); and Elaineki's father, a Tabaxi, appears (this note spells the name "Elaineke").
- **`Conditions`**: reference copy of the 5e conditions (blinded through unconscious, plus the six exhaustion levels).
