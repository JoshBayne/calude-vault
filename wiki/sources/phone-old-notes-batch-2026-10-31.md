## Evidence and methods
Each file was read in full. Frontmatter gives `created` and `source: samsung-notes`. Each `created` value falls a few seconds before its commit, so it records when the note was shared to the repository, not when it was written. The only writing date is the `1-29-24` filename, which is read as 2024-01-29 (inference).

## Limitations and caveats
- Fragments, lists and unfinished drafts. Typos are kept as typed. For example, `twilight meme video` says "gates women", probably "hates women" (inference).
- The list's "(match to my skin)" reads as Sophia's own voice, so she may have written or dictated it (inference).
- "Pop", Kori, Lydia and Seth are not identified anywhere else in the wiki. That Pop is a relative is inference.
- **Outside knowledge, not from the notes and not checked:** *Abbey in the Oakwood* is probably Caspar David Friedrich's painting, *The Fallen Angel* Alexandre Cabanel's, *Stańczyk* Jan Matejko's, *Hypnosis* Sascha Schneider's and *The Bat Woman* Albert Joseph Pénot's. *He Who Fights with Monsters* is probably the LitRPG series by Shirtaloon. *The Head of Medusa* was not identified.
- Private gift wishes and a dream about a family figure. Keep out of shared exports.
