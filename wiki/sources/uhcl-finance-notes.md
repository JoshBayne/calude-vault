# UHCL finance notes (FINC 3301, 4331, 4351)

## Summary
One substantive lecture (personal financial planning, June 2023), one short commercial-lending note (October 2023), and many empty shells. Collection page.

## Key points
- Personal finance: 50/30/20 budgeting rule; ratio definitions (savings, debt service, solvency, liquidity); Rule of 72; future value of an annuity ($3,310 for $1,000/yr, 3 yrs, 10%); Fisher equation (8% nominal, 3% inflation → 4.85% real; recomputed).
- Lending: C&I loans, EBITDA, DSCR = (EBITDA − taxes − distributions − maintenance cost) ÷ (interest + principal).

## Evidence and methods
Slide text and short worked numbers; no data tables beyond those.
