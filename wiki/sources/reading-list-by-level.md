## Limitations and caveats
- The video was not watched, and its creator and date are unknown. The list may be shortened or changed from the video.
- `created` is the share time, not the date the list was written ([[topics/phone-capture-workflow]]).
- **Name errors in the raw note** (checked against general knowledge, not against the video; the raw file is not changed):
  - "Invisible Man by Ralph Emerson": *Invisible Man* is by Ralph **Ellison**. Ralph Waldo Emerson is a different writer.
  - "Dennis Johnson": the author of *Train Dreams* is **Denis** Johnson.
  - "Katherine Patterson": the author of *Bridge to Terabithia* is Katherine **Paterson**.
  - "Madeline L'Engle": the usual spelling is **Madeleine** L'Engle.
  - "The Sun Also Rises Ernest Hemingway" has no "by"; this is only a format slip.
