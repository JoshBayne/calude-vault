# Lost Mines of Delrun campaign notes

## Summary
Session recaps (13 sessions across five in-game days), a town gazetteer for Delrun, and ~45 setting, faction and NPC pages for the principality of Arian on the continent of Belotria. `LMoP` states the published adventure was "retooled as Lost Mines of Delrun" for levels 1–5.

## Key points
- Party arcs: Cragmaw Hideout → Delrun and the Redbrands → Tresendar Manor (Glasstaff) → Thundertree and Cragmaw Castle → Wave Echo Cave (Nezznar) → Aronoc and the Disciples of Null (Golgum).
- The antagonists connect through the Sovereigns and Apostles framework: Nezznar and Golgum are both Apostles.
- Regent Prince Balan rules Arian while King Aldric sleeps unwakeably.

## Evidence and methods
Narrative recaps and worldbuilding notes; stat blocks appear only for the homebrew Disciple of Null.

## Limitations and caveats
- `Wave Echo Cave` contains long text from the published adventure; it is only briefly summarized here.
- Five player-character files (`Petals Lillydew`, `Taegan Sungem`, `Arturas Azzakod`, `Elaineki St. Germain`, `Ginevieve Ginny Hallowisp`) are empty.
- `_Monsters` (one-line index) and `_Initiative Tracker` (three PCs: Arturas 14, Petals 13, Taegan 12) were read 2026-10-29; see [[sources/empty-stub-and-unreadable-files]]. `Cady Jarman` and `Will Jarman` are about real people and are covered in [[topics/people-private]]. `Aronoc.save` and `Port of Biscayne.save` are too large for the connector.
- Session 9 is only a one-line note.
