# TikTok safe zones: 2026 guide with practical templates

## Summary
A blog guide from Zeely, a vendor of an AI ad-maker, on keeping captions, logos and calls to action visible under TikTok's interface. It defines the "safe zone" of a 1080×1920 frame, lists placement-specific overlays, and gives layout and testing habits. About a third of the page promotes Zeely's product.

## Key points
- Safe zone = the clear center of the frame once TikTok's overlays are drawn — [[concepts/tiktok-safe-zones]]
- Claimed overlay sizes: top band about 150–200 px, right icon rail about 120 px wide, bottom caption/CTA area about 250–300 px.
- Placement notes: In-Feed — keep key text about 15% above the bottom; Spark Ads — put the key message in the middle third; TopView — keep margins despite the slim playback bar; LIVE and Shopping — treat the lower third as blocked.
- Type: use medium or bold weights, about 1.3× line spacing and strong contrast, because compression blurs thin fonts.
- Process: preview in TikTok Creative Center or Ads Manager, run a pre-flight check, test on two phones, and run 2×2 tests (captions on/off, CTA top vs bottom).
- Creative advice: hook in the first 3 seconds, logo within 2 seconds, captions that echo rather than repeat the voiceover, a reusable design pack, and refresh creatives after a 20–30% performance drop.
- Accessibility: proof auto-captions by hand and pick dyslexia-friendly fonts.

## Evidence and methods
Assertion and advice, with no data of its own. It cites a HubSpot 2025 marketing report (short-form video ROI) and a Business Insider projection ($6.17B–$8.64B of ad spend at stake if TikTok's visibility dropped). Neither was opened or checked here. A screenshot credited to the TikTok Business Help Center is the only primary reference.

## Limitations and caveats
- Commercial bias: it sells an AI ad generator and says Zeely's preview "mirrors TikTok's actual UI". That is a vendor claim.
- Written for paid ads. Pixel values are approximate and may differ for organic posts and live streams. The guide does not say how TikTok's live-stream layout differs from ad layouts beyond the LIVE placement note.
- The clipping carries `created: 2026-10-29` (clip date). The page was published 2026-07-02, but an image path suggests October 2026 updates; the revision date is unknown.
