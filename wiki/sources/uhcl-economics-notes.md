# UHCL economics notes (ECON 2301/2302)

## Summary
About 45 markdown notes from Spring 2022 covering macro/micro foundations (scarcity, PPF, trade), supply and demand, elasticity, public goods, cost curves, competitive firms, monopoly, monopolistic competition and oligopoly. Collection page: it summarizes a group of files rather than one document.

## Key points
- Opportunity cost, marginal analysis (MB vs MC) and comparative advantage are the recurring tools.
- Elasticity notes include worked midpoint-method examples, a linear-demand table and a milk supply-elasticity example (all recomputed and correct).
- `Chapter 15 notes` and `Chapter 16-17` are economics despite chapter numbers that collide with accounting.

## Evidence and methods
Textbook-style definitions, numeric tables, and worked examples (Liz/Joe trade example, latte market curves, a production-and-cost table with a $10 fixed "cost of stand").
