# Video-essay series plans

## Summary
Twelve planning documents for five long video essays, each 30 minutes or more: three defences of TV endings and two critiques of stage musicals. All five are at framework stage. None has a script. The content is on [[topics/video-essay-series]]; this page records provenance and how far to trust it.

## Key points

| Essay | Stance | Runtime | Files in the vault |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Home Movies* — "The Camera Breaks" | defends the ending | 30–34 min | summary, framework |
| *Seinfeld* — "No Hugging, No Learning" | defends the ending | 30–34 min | summary, framework, citations |
| *Ed, Edd n Eddy* — "Ed... Pass It On" | defends the ending | 30–34 min | summary, framework, citations |
| *RENT* — "No Day But Today" | critique | 32–36 min | summary, framework, citations |
| *Dear Evan Hansen* — "You Deserved Consequences" | critique | 30–34 min | summary only |

- Each summary gives the thesis, the committee's decisions, a timed outline, sources, further reading and a "check before you script" list.
- Each framework expands the outline into beats, visual notes and clips.
- The likely writing order is *Home Movies*, *Seinfeld*, *Ed, Edd n Eddy*, *RENT*, *Dear Evan Hansen* (inference). The *Seinfeld* framework reuses "the Home Movies framework", the *Ed, Edd n Eddy* one refers to "the prior two", and the *Dear Evan Hansen* session in `youtube ideas` looks back at *Home Movies* and *RENT*.
- These documents continue the chat saved in `youtube ideas` ([[topics/work-and-web-projects]]), which holds the opening prompt and the *Dear Evan Hansen* deliberation.

## Evidence and methods
- All 12 files were read in full.
- The method is a five-voice "committee" (Skeptic, Fan Historian, Visual Theorist, Showrunner, Editor, as named in `youtube ideas`) that deliberates over web-search results.
- Where a summary says "your position" or "your idea", the view is the author's. That applies to the *RENT* and *Dear Evan Hansen* theses and the "Three Friends, One Outsider" beat. The rest is assistant drafting.

## Limitations and caveats
- **Nothing here was checked against the shows or the cited articles.** Facts are as the assistant reported them. No outside lookup was done for this ingest.
- **URLs were not kept.** The summaries say so. Source names survive, plus bare domains in the *Seinfeld* and *Ed, Edd n Eddy* further-reading lists.
- **Broken citation tags:** the frameworks and citation lists contain tags such as `(cite index="73-1">`. They are leftovers from the chat, and the numbers point to nothing in the vault.
- **Paraphrase inside citation tags:** the tagged text often reads as the assistant's paraphrase, so it should not be treated as a direct quote.
- **Self-reported gaps:** each summary ends with a list of unverified items, such as general-knowledge facts and the assistant's own arguments. These are carried over to [[topics/video-essay-series]].
- **Internal conflicts:** the documents disagree in a few places, such as carjacking against mugging in *Seinfeld*. These are listed on the topic page.
- **Weak sources:** many sources are fan wikis, Tumblr posts, forums and Medium posts. The documents themselves say these should not be shown on screen as authority.
- **Missing files:** *Home Movies* has no separate citation list, and *Dear Evan Hansen* has neither a framework file nor a citation list. Its summary carries both in short form.
- **Dates:** the files carry no chat dates. They reached the vault on 2026-11-01. The *Seinfeld* plan discusses a 2024 finale, so the chats are from 2024 or later.
