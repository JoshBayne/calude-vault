# Personal notes (daily notes 2021–2022, hobbies)

## Summary
Journal entries and weekly planning pages from December 2021 to 2022, plus hobby notes: a guitar practice log (Aug–Sep 2024) and learning-level roadmap (Sept 2025), a vinyl wishlist, Gundam diorama plans, 3D-print and keyboard reference notes.

## Key points
- The weekly template has Meal Plan, Due Dates, Goals and Week in Review sections.
- Guitar log: chord-change targets of 30/min then 60/min; best logged scores 39/min (A–D, D–E) and 50/min (Am–Em).
- Journal text is now recorded in full detail in [[topics/daily-notes-2022]] (private).
