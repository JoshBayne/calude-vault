# Sci-fi setting notes (United Interstellar Coalition)

## Summary
A space-opera setting: ancient warp gates discovered after the early-2020s JWST launch; humanity governed by the UIC for about 500 years; a loose criminal federation (The Clan); and a story thread about a researcher protecting an AI called AI-ex.

## Key points
- A "Montauk Event" is any gate-error disappearance, named after the lost UINSC Montauk, and these are becoming more frequent.
- The UIC is a bloated bureaucracy led by a Council of 13 with Headhunters as a private peacekeeping force.
- The Clan's cells operate independently under a protection pact: when one issues an APB, all must honor it.

## Evidence and methods
Prose lore pages and short scenes; no mechanics.

## Limitations and caveats
- About 15 pages (`Captain-Elias-Windsor`, `Chobo`, `Dogu`, `Mars`, `Gat`, `Soren`, `Steph`, `Still-Waters`, `The-Royale`, `Crimson-Empire-Invades`, `Secret-Society-Meddling`, `Accidental-Sacrifice`, `AI-ex`, `United-Interstellar-Coalition`, duplicates) are ink drawings with no extractable text; `TVOM(2)` is a PDF link of unknown subject.
