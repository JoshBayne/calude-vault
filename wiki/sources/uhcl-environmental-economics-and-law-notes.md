# UHCL environmental economics and business law notes

## Summary
Two groups. (1) **Energy and Environmental Economics** (Spring 2024): exam-1 and exam-2 study guides, an exam-style note, and an electricity-market presentation (zonal vs nodal pricing in Texas). (2) **Business law**: `Exam 1 Review` (legal system, constitutional law, ethics) and `Exam 2 Review` (IP, cyberlaw, crime, contracts, international law); course and term not stated.

## Key points
- Environmental economics: positive vs normative claims, static efficiency, property rights (exclusivity, transferability, enforceability), negative externalities and deadweight loss, point vs nonpoint pollution, fund vs stock pollutants.
- Presentation notes: nodal pricing raised coal operating costs and CO₂ in the study described, while zonal pricing created gaming incentives; daily CO₂ rose 5.5% (as recorded in the note; the underlying study was not examined).
- Law: patents 20 years; trademark renewal between years 5–6 then every 10 years; CAN-SPAM Act of 2003; ACPA and typosquatting; UCC cover and cure.

## Evidence and methods
Study-guide outlines; the presentation notes paraphrase a single study.

## Limitations and caveats
- Only the opening ~3,000 characters of `Exam-2-Review` (environmental economics) were read in full; the rest was skimmed.
- `HW2`, `Week-*` and `Review-Links` are link-only or empty. `Exam-3-review` (2022) is an unrelated empty stub.
- Legal statements are the author's study notes, not verified against current law.
