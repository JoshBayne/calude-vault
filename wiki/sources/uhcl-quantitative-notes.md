# UHCL quantitative notes (MATH 1324, DSCI 3321, DSCI 3331)

## Summary
Business math (functions, linear equations, systems, break-even, quadratics; Spring 2022), statistics (t-based confidence intervals, sample size, hypothesis tests, paired t test; Fall 2022) and quantitative analysis (QA approach, decision analysis; DSCI 3331).

## Key points
- Break-even and profit functions recur: `Chapter 1.6` profit examples and the "Bagels R Us" break-even (200 bagels).
- Statistics examples recompute correctly: sample size n = 323 for p = 0.7, E = 0.05, 95% confidence; paired t = 2.63 vs critical 2.179; 98% CI [26.29, 54.83].
- Decision analysis payoffs (Thompson's sheds) give EVPI of 60,000 by recomputation.

## Evidence and methods
Worked numeric examples from slides, OCR'd from images; the author's own recomputations are labeled on [[topics/statistics]] and [[topics/math-1324-business-math]].

## Limitations and caveats
- **Errors in the source notes** (documented on the math topic page): `Chapter 1.6` break-even writes fixed cost as 3600 and 36000 in different lines (answers x > 120 and P(200) = 24,000 fit 36,000; 3,600 would give x > 12 and 56,400; internally inconsistent, textbook not checked); minor slips in `Chapter 1.1`, `1.6` (supply equation +20 vs +30) and `2.1`.
- `Chapter 1.4 Graphing Utilities` is empty; `1324-final-exam-review-proble` and the syllabus PDFs were not read.
- The two retirement and policy-survey hypothesis examples were set up in slides without visible answers; the z-values are my computation.
