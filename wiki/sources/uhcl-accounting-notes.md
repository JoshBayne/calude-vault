# UHCL accounting notes (ACCT 2301, 2302, 3341)

## Summary
Three accounting courses: introductory financial accounting (Spring 2022, NetSolutions examples), managerial accounting (Fall 2022; cost classification, job-order costing, decentralized performance), and intermediate accounting (Summer 2023; inventory valuation, quizzes, reviews). Collection page.

## Key points
- Double-entry rules, journal → ledger → trial balance, and error-detection heuristics (differences divisible by 9 indicate transpositions).
- Predetermined overhead rate (50,000 ÷ 10,000 DLH = $5/DLH), under/overapplied overhead closed to COGS.
- Ch. 24 exercises on DuPont ROI, residual income and transfer pricing (recomputed: total company gain $2.2M on 40,000 units; negotiated range $125–$180).
- Intermediate-accounting slides: NRV and lower-of-cost-or-NRV (Whole Foods item example sums to $384,000), quizzes 1–2 answer keys.

## Evidence and methods
Worked journal entries, T-accounts, trial balances, numeric exercises; numbers were recomputed where noted on the topic pages.

## Limitations and caveats
- **Data-quality flags**: the NetSolutions trial balance in `2-4 Trial Balance` sums to 42,690 in credits against a stated 42,600 (likely Fees Earned 16,430 vs 16,340); `First Spreadsheet Notes` shows an Inventory credit of 8,750 against a 4,375 journal entry; `2-3` is an unfinished stub.
- Image-only or link-only files: `Lean-Accounting-Notes`, `Ch-25/26/27`, most `Chapter-N-PowerPoint` shells, and class-exercise PDFs. `Homework-2---Questions(2)` (~120 KB) not summarized.
