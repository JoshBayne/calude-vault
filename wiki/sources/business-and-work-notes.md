# Business and work notes (SMPP website, AI transcripts)

## Summary
December 2021 page-by-page plans for a children's character-entertainment business website, plus saved AI-assistant conversations on a pricing calculator, a character lightbox page, Notifuse/Square messaging, SharePoint and Microsoft 365, file scripting, SOC 2 career mapping and a video-essay prompt.

## Key points
- Site plan: hero banner, review cards, gallery, FAQ, pricing, character index with ~18 character pages, and a cast page.
- `dynamic pricing` asks for a calculator for up to 4 performers, four time blocks (30 min to 3+ hr) and three character types.
- `notifuse project`: no native Square connector exists; the proposed path is middleware receiving Square webhooks.
- `compliance goals`: asks how a BS in Finance maps to compliance work such as SOC 2.

## Evidence and methods
Design notes and AI conversation transcripts; assistant answers are not independently verified.
