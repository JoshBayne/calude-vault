# UHCL communication and miscellaneous notes

## Summary
Public-speaking course notes (Spring 2022): key terms from Chapters 2–4 and 9–11, assignment rules for speeches 1 and 3, and a final-speech terms sheet. Also small unrelated course notes grouped here for lack of a better home.

## Key points
- Speech purposes (ceremonial, demonstration, epideictic, informative, forensic, deliberative, persuasive) and fallacies (ad populum, hasty generalization, post-hoc, slippery slope).
- Speech 1 is a 3–5 minute "I am an advocate for…" talk; Speech 3 is 4–5 minutes on online vs face-to-face life with 2–3 citations.
- Misc: emotional intelligence has four domains (`Chapter 2 - Thriving in Business`); services differ from products in how quality and capacity are managed (`Operations`); `Week-2` lists medieval-history readings and pre-class questions.

## Evidence and methods
Definition lists and assignment prompts; no recordings or drafts.
