# Nezznar the Black Spider

## Overview
A drow Apostle who was restarting the Wave Echo Cave mine to forge weapons; the main villain of the Delrun arc.

## Key facts
- Founded the Redbrands via Glasstaff (Iarno Albreck) to supply slave labor — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Killed Tharden Rockseeker; Gundren and Nundro were rescued by the party — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Defeated in sessions 9–10; the notes describe him as reborn as a spider-drow monstrosity before the fight — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The doppelganger Vyerith was loyal to him and remains at large — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
