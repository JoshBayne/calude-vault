# Wave Echo Cave

## Overview
A lost mine fifteen miles east of [[entities/delrun]], in the Sword Mountains. In the campaign it is where the party faces [[entities/nezznar-the-black-spider]], who was restarting the mine to forge weapons.

## Key facts
- The **Delrundian Pact** is an agreement among the dwarves, gnomes and humans of the Delrun area, made hundreds of years ago, to jointly operate a mine here with equal access to its **Forge of Spells** — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The published adventure text calls it "the rich mine of the Phandelver's Pact", lost five hundred years ago during orc invasions. The campaign's own name for the agreement is the Delrundian Pact — raw `Wave Echo Cave` and `Delrundian Pact`
- Sessions 9–10 explore it and defeat Nezznar; the party recovers the Lightbringer mace and Dragonguard armor and rescues Nundro — [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]]
- After the fight, session 11 settles a deal for 10% of future mine profits — [[topics/dnd-campaigns-and-worldbuilding]]
- The `Wave Echo Cave` source file is mostly long published-adventure text (a 20-area dungeon), so it is not evidence of original worldbuilding — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
