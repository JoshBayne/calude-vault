# Disciples of Null

## Overview
A religious order in Aronoc whose members were turned into vampires by Golgum to take the city for the Sovereigns.

## Key facts
- Leaders: Bishop Vlodimire, Sister Nora, Father Ubik, Father Conrad — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- A homebrew "Disciple of Null" stat block (CR 1, undead) mixes zombie "Loathsome Limbs" with vampire regeneration, spider climb and a 3/day enslave — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The Religious Library keeper Medrash explains a dark cult of Null, "sovereign of darkness and emptiness", and that the casket carvings depict the last "Ceremony of the Eclipse" — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- In session 13 the party sees a priest turning into a vampire and concludes the plague is vampiric — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
