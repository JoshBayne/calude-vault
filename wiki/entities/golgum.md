# Golgum

## Overview
An Apostle: a being that made a deal with the Sovereigns to exist outside causality. He appears as a suave young elf with amber eyes; his true form is a man-bat whose venom makes vampire slaves, who in turn make zombies.

## Key facts
- Poses as a priest in Lathander's temple in Aronoc, feeding on the ill and infirm and plotting to enslave the city — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Refuses audiences except through the Council, referring the party to Bishop Vlodimire or Sister Nora of the Disciples of Null — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- A casket intended for him was intercepted by the party (session 11); it carries necromantic dirt — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Reportedly made the Disciples of Null vampires to take Aronoc for the Sovereigns — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
