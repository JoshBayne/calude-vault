# Delrun

## Overview
A frontier town in the Emerald Wood region, rebuilt over an ancient city that pre-dates the Delrundian Pact. It is the base for the campaign's first adventure, retooled from *Lost Mine of Phandelver* as "Lost Mines of Delrun".

## Key facts
- Has no Abiding Hand protection; locations include the Townmaster's Hall, Stonehill Inn (Toblen Stonehill), Barthen's Provisions, the Lionshield Coster, Edermath Orchard and Tresendar Manor — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The Redbrands, a gang founded by Glasstaff to find slave labor for the Black Spider, controlled the area until the party defeated them (sessions 3–5) — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The party can purchase Tresendar Manor — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Wave Echo Cave lies fifteen miles east, in the Sword Mountains — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Ginevieve "Ginny" Hallowisp stayed in Delrun to help restart the mine — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
