# Aronoc

## Overview
A coastal city in the principality of Arian, nicknamed the "City of Lights", and the setting for the late sessions of the campaign (sessions 11–13).

## Key facts
- Five districts: Dwarven (fabrication), Trade, North (seat of power; the Measured Knightstead magic school), Old District (poor, many orphans), and The Spires (temples of Eldath, Helm and Lathander) — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- The Spires' Religious Library is kept by Medrash Nommonis, a dragonborn cleric of Helm — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Gnome professor Zaffrab Symphony, the Knightstead's only active teacher, unlocks the casket found in a cart meant for Golgum — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Golgum poses as a priest in Lathander's temple and plots to enslave the city — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
- Arturas Azzakod fled the city; Thin Pork's Athena Luckytwist was a parental figure to him — [[sources/delrun-campaign-notes]]
