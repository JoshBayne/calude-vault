# United Interstellar Coalition (UIC)

## Overview
The main governing body of humanity and its worlds in the sci-fi setting, descended from the old Earth United Nations and in power for about 500 years.

## Key facts
- Headquartered on Mars; keeps peace through the UIC Navy — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
- Led by the Council of 13, which uses Headhunters as a private peacekeeping force — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
- A bloated bureaucracy that cannot stop corruption among its upper ranks — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
- The lost exploration ship UINSC Montauk belonged to the UIC; gate-error disappearances are called Montauk Events — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
