# Dr. Harrison Nguyen

## Overview
A kind, honest government researcher on Chobo who built systems to help map the warp gates, ending with the AI "AI-ex", whom he came to see as a daughter.

## Key facts
- Ordered to hand the AI to the government, he chose to run, using the character Steph to obtain forged papers for another world's visa terminals and a captain who rents out a ship — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
- He depends on Gat to get him safely off-world — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]
- In the story-beat notes a cagey man arrives at a starport motel with a young child; he reveals he is a government researcher disgusted that the government uses AIs as slave labor — [[sources/sci-fi-setting-notes]]. The notes do not say outright that this man is Nguyen (inference).
